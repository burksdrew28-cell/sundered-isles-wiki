# Sir Pugsington

_Knight of Solcrest • Royal Paladin • Very Good Boy_

Sir Pugsington is a small awakened pug dressed in meticulously fitted plate armor bearing the insignia of Solcrest. His stout frame, wrinkled face, and occasional involuntary snort contrast spectacularly with the dignity with which he carries himself. He walks with the confidence of a knight ten times his size and seems entirely unaware that anyone might find his appearance unusual.

Pugsington is courageous, honorable, stubborn, and deeply compassionate. He takes knighthood extraordinarily seriously, particularly when it comes to protecting those being mistreated. Despite his formal demeanor, he has a warm heart and a subtle mischievous streak that occasionally breaks through his knightly composure. He dislikes bullies, unnecessary cruelty, corruption, and anyone who forgets the **"Sir."**

<details> <summary><strong>✨ Special Abilities</strong></summary>

### Divine Smite

When Pugsington hits a creature with a melee weapon attack, he may expend a spell slot to deal additional radiant damage.

- **1st-Level Slot:** +2d8 radiant
    
- **2nd-Level Slot:** +3d8 radiant
    

Deals an additional **1d8 radiant damage** against undead or fiends.

### Lay on Paws

Pugsington possesses a pool of **30 healing points**.

As an action, he can touch a creature and restore any number of hit points remaining in the pool.

Alternatively, he may expend 5 points to cure one disease or neutralize one poison.

### Aura of Protection

Friendly creatures within **10 feet** of Pugsington gain **+3 to saving throws** while he is conscious.

### Divine Sense

Pugsington can magically sense powerful celestial, fiendish, or undead presences within 60 feet.

**Uses:** 4 per long rest.

### Keen Smell

Pugsington has **advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on smell.**

Yes. This is occasionally far more useful than anyone expects.

### Brave Little Knight

Pugsington has **advantage on saving throws against being frightened.**

# 🧠 DM Information

<details> <summary><strong>🎯 Goals & Motivations</strong></summary>

### Immediate Goal

Ensure [[Princess Seleni]]'s wedding celebrations proceed safely while assisting the overwhelmed guards and citizens of [[Solcrest]].

During the gate scene, he becomes interested in the party after witnessing their trouble entering the city and ultimately **uses his authority to sponsor their entry.**

### Long-Term Goal

Protect Solcrest and its people while living up to the ideals he believes knighthood represents.

As the campaign develops, this extends beyond Solcrest. Pugsington increasingly sees protecting the party and helping uncover threats to the Sundered Isles as part of his duty.

### Motivation

Pugsington genuinely believes that authority exists to **serve and protect people**, not simply control them.

He admires courage, compassion, loyalty, and people willing to help strangers.

### Fear

Failing someone who trusted him to protect them.

His deepest fear is being forced to choose between **loyalty to those he loves and doing what he believes is morally right.**

</details>

# 🎲 Encounter Notes

<details> <summary><strong>⚔️ Combat Tactics</strong></summary>

### Before Combat

Pugsington attempts diplomacy first unless civilians or companions are already in immediate danger.

If combat appears unavoidable, he positions himself between the threat and whichever party member appears most vulnerable.

### Opening Move

Usually casts **Bless** or **Shield of Faith** rather than immediately attacking.

Against an obvious major threat, he may instead charge directly into melee to prevent the enemy from reaching the party.

### During Combat

Pugsington functions primarily as a **tank/support ally**.

Priority:

1. Protect unconscious or vulnerable party members.
    
2. Heal allies in danger.
    
3. Keep dangerous enemies occupied.
    
4. Buff the party.
    
5. Attack and use Divine Smite when necessary.
    

**Do not have Pugsington constantly take killing blows.**

Whenever possible, his actions should create opportunities for the **players** to do something heroic.

### At Half HP

Pugsington becomes more determined rather than frightened.

He may stop conserving spell slots and begin using Divine Smite more aggressively.

If another character is badly injured, he prioritizes protecting them even at significant personal risk.

### Retreat / Surrender

Pugsington will retreat if remaining would accomplish nothing and doing so allows him to protect others.

He is extremely reluctant to abandon:

- Civilians
    
- An unconscious companion
    
- Someone under his protection
    

He will surrender if doing so saves innocent lives.

</details>

<details> <summary><strong>💬 Social Encounter Notes</strong></summary>

- **Persuasion DC:** 12 for reasonable requests; 16 for requests conflicting with duty
    
- **Deception DC:** 15
    
- **Intimidation DC:** 18
    
- **Insight DC:** 12 to recognize when his formal demeanor is hiding concern
    

### Helpful

Pugsington responds extremely well to:

- Protecting innocent people
    
- Showing compassion
    
- Honoring promises
    
- Standing up against unfair treatment
    
- Treating him as an actual knight
    
- Acts of bravery that do not involve unnecessary cruelty
    

### Hostile

Pugsington strongly dislikes:

- Cruelty
    
- Bullying
    
- Corruption
    
- Cowardly attacks against helpless creatures
    
- Deliberately endangering civilians
    
- Mocking his knighthood after being corrected
    

Calling him "doggy" is not technically a crime.

His expression may suggest otherwise.

### Leverage

Appeals involving innocent lives, honor, duty, or protecting Solcrest are extremely effective.

He is much harder to manipulate through wealth or personal gain.

</details>

<details> <summary><strong>🪝 Quests & Plot Hooks</strong></summary>

- Pugsington can serve as the party's first meaningful connection to Solcrest and the royal household.
    
- He can send the party to investigate problems that seem too unusual or politically delicate for ordinary guards.
    
- He may notice inconsistencies in official explanations surrounding magical or crystal-related events.
    
- His access to the castle can eventually expose the party to information normally hidden from common citizens.
    
- His loyalty to the royal family can become complicated as the party uncovers the truth behind the history of the Sundered Isles.
    
- Pugsington can act as a recurring ally throughout the campaign without permanently traveling with the party.
    

</details>
