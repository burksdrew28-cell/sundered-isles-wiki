_King of Astra Veyra_

> **Quick Read:** Calm, controlled ruler who genuinely wants to protect Astra Veyra, but carries dangerous pieces of a history the kingdom was never meant to remember.

---

## 👤 Basics

- **Species:** Human
    
- **Occupation:** King of Astra Veyra
    
- **Home:** [[Solcrest]]
    
- **Affiliation:** [[Royal Family of Solcrest]]
    
- **Status:** Alive
    

---

## 🎭 Roleplay

**Personality:** Composed, intelligent, authoritative, guarded

**Voice:** Slow, deliberate, formal; rarely raises his voice

**Mannerism:** Pauses before answering difficult questions and maintains intense eye contact

**Wants:** Preserve the stability of Astra Veyra and protect his family

**Attitude Toward Party:** Neutral / Observant

---

## 🧠 DM Knowledge

- Knows **fragments of the truth surrounding [[The Sundering]]**.
    
- Knows the royal bloodline has deliberately protected and suppressed certain ancient information.
    
- Does **not** possess the complete truth himself.
    
- Will avoid discussing restricted history unless circumstances force him to.
    
- May become increasingly wary of the party as they uncover things they should not know.
