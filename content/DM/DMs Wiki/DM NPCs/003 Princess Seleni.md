_Princess of Astra Veyra_

> **Quick Read:** Warm, intelligent princess caught between royal expectations and her desire to understand the world beyond the castle.

- **Species:** Human
    
- **Occupation:** Princess of Astra Veyra
    
- **Home:** [[Solcrest]]
    
- **Affiliation:** [[Royal Family of Solcrest]]
    
- **Status:** Alive
    

---

## 🎭 Roleplay

**Personality:** Warm, curious, clever, compassionate

**Voice:** Friendly and articulate; noticeably less formal once comfortable

**Mannerism:** Asks genuine questions about people rather than simply making polite conversation

**Wants:** Fulfill her responsibilities while retaining some control over her own life

**Attitude Toward Party:** Friendly / Curious

---

## 🧠 DM Knowledge

- Knows considerably less about the royal family's hidden history than [[King Elon]].
    
- Is largely unaware of the truth surrounding [[The Sundering]].
    
- Has picked up plenty of court gossip and political knowledge.
    
- Could become suspicious of her father if the party exposes contradictions in official history.
    
- Can eventually become an important bridge between the party and the royal family.
