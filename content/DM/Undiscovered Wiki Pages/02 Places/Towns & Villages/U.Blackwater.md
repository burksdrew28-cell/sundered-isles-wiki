Hidden along a rough stretch of coast, **[[Blackwater]]** is a small pirate settlement of crooked docks, patched ships, loud taverns, and people who generally prefer that authorities forget the place exists. Smugglers, privateers, sailors, and questionable merchants regularly pass through, making [[Blackwater]] an excellent place to find goods that never appeared on any official shipping record. Despite its shabby reputation, the village maintains surprisingly strong connections with **[[Ilyara]]** to the south, and those who know the right people can arrange passage, trade, or information across the southern seas faster here than almost anywhere else in [[Astra Veyra]].
