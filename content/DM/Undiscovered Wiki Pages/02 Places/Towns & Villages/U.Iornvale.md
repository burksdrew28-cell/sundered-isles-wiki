Far in northeastern [[Iornveil]] lies **Iornvale**, a small settlement surrounded by rolling hills and distant mountain peaks. Known for grazing animals, hardy crops, and unusually rich soil deposited from the surrounding highlands. Life moves slowly in Iornvale, and locals measure distance less in miles than in how many hills you have to cross to get there.
