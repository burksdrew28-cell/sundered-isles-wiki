Hidden among the remote northern highlands, **Ashvale** is a quiet village tucked into one of [[Iornveil]]’s most isolated corners. Small stone homes cluster around patches of dark soil where hardy crops grow surprisingly well, while thin columns of chimney smoke seem to hang permanently between the surrounding hills. Few travelers arrive without deliberately seeking Ashvale, and the locals appear perfectly content with that arrangement.
