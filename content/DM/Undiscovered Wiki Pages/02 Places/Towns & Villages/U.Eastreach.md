Sitting on the outer edge of [[The Marshlands]], **[[Eastreach]]** is a small village of raised cottages, narrow boardwalks, reed fields, and muddy roads that seem to change shape after every heavy rain. The people here make a living harvesting marsh plants, trapping small game, and guiding travelers along the safer routes through the wetlands. [[Eastreach]] has a reputation for strange local superstitions, and nearly every doorway is decorated with bundles of dried herbs, carved charms, or little bells meant to keep “unwanted things” from following travelers home.
