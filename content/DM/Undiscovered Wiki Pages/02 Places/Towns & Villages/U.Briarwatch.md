Hidden beneath the towering sea cliffs of [[Eladrion]]'s plateau, **[[Briarwatch]]** is a secluded coastal settlement squeezed between the rock face and the crashing ocean. Most of the village is built from dark stone and driftwood, with homes tucked into natural caves or sheltered beneath enormous cliff overhangs to escape the violent coastal weather. [[Briarwatch]] is best known for its cliffside signal fires and skilled wave-watchers, who study the tides, storms, and migrating sea creatures from narrow lookout platforms carved into the rock. Reaching the village by land requires navigating a steep switchback trail down the cliffs, which is precisely how the locals prefer it.
