**Lunaris** is a graceful elven city built among warm coastal forests, pale stone terraces, and shallow waterways flowing toward the sea. The city is especially known for its astronomy, navigation, and glasswork, with tall observatories rising above the treetops and mirrored towers catching sunlight during the day. At night, hundreds of lanterns illuminate bridges, gardens, and canals, giving Lunaris its reputation as one of the most beautiful cities in Velora.

The elves of Lunaris are curious, meticulous, and deeply fascinated by the movement of stars, tides, and seasons. Sailors from across [[The Sundered Isles]] seek out Lunarian navigators, while artisans produce delicate lenses, maps, instruments, and colored glass found in wealthy homes far beyond [[Velora]]. Despite its warm climate and lush surroundings, Lunaris has a surprisingly scholarly atmosphere, where even casual conversations have a habit of becoming debates about constellations, currents, or whether someone’s map is three degrees wrong.
