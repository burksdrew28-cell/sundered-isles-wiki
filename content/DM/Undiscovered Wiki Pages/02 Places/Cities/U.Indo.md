**[[Indo]]** is the great cargo gateway of [[Astra Veyra]], a hard-working port city through which enormous amounts of foreign goods enter the continent. Its docks operate nearly without pause, filled with towering cranes, cargo ships, warehouses, shouting foremen, exhausted sailors, and crews moving crates faster than most visitors can keep track of. Spices, metals, fabrics, food, tools, luxury goods, and countless stranger imports pass through [[Indo]] before spreading across [[Astra Veyra]].

The people of [[Indo]] have a reputation for being tough, impatient, and extremely uninterested in anyone slowing down their work. Lost travelers wandering through the dock districts are more likely to be pointed toward the nearest exit than politely escorted anywhere. Despite its rough exterior, [[Indo]] is one of the most important economic centers on the continent, and fortunes can be made here quickly, assuming you know what you are doing. If you do not, [[Indo]] has several excellent ways of teaching you.
