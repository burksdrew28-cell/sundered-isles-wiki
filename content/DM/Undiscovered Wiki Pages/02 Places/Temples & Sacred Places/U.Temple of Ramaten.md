High in the [[Yarto Mountains]] near the source of the **[[Gem River]]** stands the ancient **Temple of Ramaten**, a dwarven sanctuary built directly into a towering wall of stone. Pilgrims, miners, warriors, and craftspeople travel here to leave offerings and seek blessings before major undertakings, with thousands of names and messages carved into the surrounding rock by generations of visitors. Exactly how old the temple is remains uncertain, though dwarven tradition claims worship has taken place on the mountain since long before the surrounding settlements existed.

---

- **Location:** [[Yarto Mountains]]
- **Associated Faith / Deity:** [[Ramaten]]
- **Known For:** Pilgrimages, blessings, ancient dwarven traditions
