_Knight of Solcrest • Paladin • Very Good Boy_

Sir Pugsington is a small but unmistakably proud pug clad in finely fitted plate armor, often seen marching through [[Solcrest]] with the confidence of a knight three times his size. His squashed face, stout build, and occasional snort might undermine the grandeur of his appearance if he did not carry himself with such absolute dignity.

Known for his courage, courtesy, and willingness to help those he believes deserve it, Pugsington has become a familiar figure around Solcrest. Though some newcomers initially mistake him for an unusually well-dressed pet, locals know better. **Sir Pugsington is a knight, and he expects to be addressed accordingly.**
