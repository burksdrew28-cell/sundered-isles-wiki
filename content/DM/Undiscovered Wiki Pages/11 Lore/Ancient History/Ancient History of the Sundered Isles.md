Much of the earliest history of **[[The Sundered Isles]]** has been lost to time. Ruins scattered across the continents suggest that great civilizations existed thousands of years before many modern kingdoms, with ancient roads, buried cities, forgotten fortresses, and structures whose original purposes remain unknown. Elven records, dwarven carvings, oral traditions, and surviving fragments of old texts occasionally offer glimpses into this distant age, but they rarely agree with one another. Some describe powerful kingdoms spanning lands that are now separated by sea, while others speak of creatures, magic, and places that modern scholars dismiss as exaggeration or myth.

What is known with greater certainty is that the ancient world eventually collapsed during a period of enormous conflict and upheaval. Entire settlements disappeared, borders changed, civilizations fractured, and countless records were destroyed. The modern calendar begins at the end of this era, with **Year 0** marking the point from which the history of the present-day Sundered Isles is traditionally measured. More than six thousand years have passed since then, and for most people today these events belong more to legend than history. Ancient ruins are plentiful, answers are not, and every culture seems to possess its own version of what the world looked like before everything changed.
