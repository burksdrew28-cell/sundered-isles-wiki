Ancient records describe a devastating conflict that spread across much of the known world sometime before **Year 0**. Who fought, what began the war, and even how long it lasted remain heavily debated, as surviving accounts contradict one another and often mix history with mythology. Stories speak of armies wielding extraordinary magic, cities destroyed in moments, strange creatures appearing upon battlefields, and catastrophes that permanently reshaped the land. The war ended around Year 0, but exactly **how** it ended has been lost. In modern times, scholars generally treat the conflict as the dividing line between the ancient world and the beginning of recorded history, while most ordinary people simply know it as the distant events from which the Sundered Isles eventually emerged.
