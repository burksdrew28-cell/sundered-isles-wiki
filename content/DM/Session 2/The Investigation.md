# 🎲 Session 2: The Aether Bloom

**In-Game Date:** 3 Highsun, 6174 AS, 2 weeks after The Shattering event

**Starting Location:** Solcrest, Astra Veyra

**Party Level:** 3 (5 Players)

**Expected Session Length:** 4–6 Hours

---

## 🎯 Session Overview

### Main Objective:

Sir Pugsington sends the party to investigate a mysterious impact site near Solcrest after a patrol of Royal Guards disappears.

The party must investigate the crater, discover what happened to the missing soldiers, and retrieve whatever fell from the sky.

### Important Story Developments:

- King Elon remains missing 2 weeks after the disaster.
- Mysterious impacts and strange phenomena have been reported throughout the Sundered Isles.
- The Solcrest crater has experienced an unstable Aether Bloom.
- Missing Royal Guards have transformed into crystal-worshipping cultists.
- Captain Charlie has become a heavily mutated cultist leader.
- The hooded Fey woman from Session 1 is using an ancient machine to amplify the crystal fragment's power.
- The party experiences another collective vision from the mysterious entity.
- The Solcrest archivist believes the scattered crystal fragments must be recovered to restore stability.

### Expected Ending / Cliffhanger:

The party returns to Solcrest with the recovered crystal fragment.

Sir Pugsington introduces them to the city archivist, who believes that collecting and reuniting the scattered fragments may prevent further instability.

The party must decide where to travel next.

**Suggested Destination:** Tulin, due to rumors concerning King Elon and its importance as a transportation hub.

- The party arrived in Solcrest for Princess Seleni's wedding.
- Captain Charlie took their attempted bribe but refused them entry.
- Sir Pugsington helped the party enter the city.

Each of you has spent the past two weeks helping the city recover or pursuing your own interests.

Yet the memories of that day linger. The shattered crystal. The mysterious voice. The choices you made.

And in the quiet moments, certain thoughts refuse to leave you...

## 🗺️ 2. Scene & Encounter Flow

## Scene 1: Sir Pugsington's Summons

**Location:** Solcrest

**Purpose:** Introduce the mission, missing patrol, and reports of strange events throughout the Sundered Isles.

### What Happens:

- Sir Pugsington summons the party after their two weeks of downtime.
- Reports have arrived that strange objects fell from the sky throughout the Sundered Isles.
- Unusual phenomena have been reported near several impact sites, although details remain unclear.
- Pugsington sent twelve Royal Guards to investigate the crater near Solcrest. Only one returned.
- The surviving soldier is disoriented and unable to explain exactly what happened.
- Pugsington asks the party to investigate the crater, discover what happened to the missing soldiers, and retrieve whatever fell from the sky.
- He offers the party a reward for completing the mission.

**Mission Objectives:**

1. Investigate the impact site.
2. Discover what happened to the missing Royal Guards.
3. Retrieve whatever fell from the sky, if possible.
4. Bring back any unusual objects or important information.

### Descriptions / Atmosphere:

> Sir Pugsington approaches you with an unusually serious expression.
> 
> "My friends, I'm afraid I must ask for your assistance once again."
> 
> "Since the disaster, reports have been arriving from across the Isles. Objects falling from the sky, strange changes in the land, and occurrences that our scholars cannot explain."
> 
> "I sent twelve of our finest soldiers to investigate the impact site outside Solcrest."
> 
> "Only one returned."
> 
> "I need you to discover what happened to the others. And whatever it was that fell from the sky, bring it back if you can."

### NPCs / Enemies:

- Sir Pugsington
- Surviving Royal Guard (optional interaction)

### Player Choices / Alternate Outcomes:

**Persuasion DC 12:** Convince Pugsington to provide additional supplies or information about the patrol.

**Insight DC 11:** Recognize that Pugsington is particularly concerned about the surviving soldier's condition.

**Persuasion DC 13:** Calm the surviving soldier enough for him to recall what happened.

He remembers the soldiers gathering around something beautiful, hearing a strange humming sound, and someone saying they needed to move it north.

**Medicine DC 12:** Discover that the surviving soldier appears unusually healthy. Old injuries and scars have seemingly disappeared.

**Optional Reward:** Additional healing potions, basic supplies, or information about the patrol's last known location.

### Transition to Next Scene:

The party leaves Solcrest and travels toward the reported impact site.

## Scene 4: The Withered Crater

**Location:** Solcrest impact site

**Purpose:** Reveal that the fallen crystal fragment has been removed and establish the unstable nature of the Aether Bloom.

### What Happens:

- The crater is nearly barren.
- Evidence suggests that enormous vegetation once flourished here before rapidly withering.
- Huge dead flowers have collapsed along the crater walls.
- Crystal-veined roots stretch across the ground.
- Dead insects and strange plant matter cover the soil.
- The center contains an empty impact cavity.
- Large drag marks lead north, accompanied by numerous humanoid footprints.

**DM Secret:** The missing Royal Guards dragged the crystal fragment from the crater to Stormhold Ruins. Its removal caused the Aether Bloom surrounding the crater to collapse.

### Descriptions / Atmosphere:

> The crater is surrounded by enormous, withered flowers.
> 
> Their once-luminous petals have faded into translucent husks, while twisted roots emerge from the broken earth.
> 
> Dead insects lie scattered across the ground, their bodies still emitting a faint glow.
> 
> At the center is a deep cavity surrounded by shattered stone.
> 
> Whatever once occupied this space is gone.
> 
> Deep grooves cut through the soil, leading up the northern slope and disappearing into the trees.

### NPCs / Enemies:

No major combat required.

Optional environmental hazard: Unstable spores.

### Player Choices / Alternate Outcomes:

**Investigation DC 10:** Determine that a large object was dragged from the center of the crater.

**Survival DC 11:** Follow the drag marks north.

**Survival DC 14:** Identify numerous humanoid tracks accompanying the object. Some tracks display unusual proportions.

**Nature DC 12:** Determine that the surrounding vegetation grew and died at an unnatural rate.

**Arcana DC 14:** Detect lingering magical energy flowing through the roots toward the north.

**Perception DC 13:** Notice distant ruined stone walls on a hill beyond the trees.

**Optional Environmental Hazard: Unstable Spores**

Disturbing a large cluster of dead flowers releases a cloud of magical spores.

**Constitution Saving Throw DC 12**

- Success: No damage.
- Failure: 1d4 poison damage and a brief hallucination of the crater in full bloom.

### Transition to Next Scene:

The drag marks, humanoid footprints, or distant ruined walls lead the party north toward Stormhold Ruins.

## Scene 15: The Ancient Archivist

**Location:** Solcrest Library, archivist's workshop

**Purpose:** Introduce a recurring lore NPC, connect the events of Sessions 1 and 2, and establish the theory that the crystal fragments must be reunited.

### What Happens:

- Sir Pugsington brings the party to the city archivist.
- The archivist is an approximately 800-year-old humanoid construct who serves as a scholar, artificer, and historical advisor.
- His workshop contains ancient books, mechanical components, unusual artifacts, and crystal instruments.
- He is a close friend and trusted advisor of King Elon.
- Pugsington introduces the party and explains that they recovered a mysterious object from the impact site.
- The archivist examines the crystal fragment and recognizes that it is unlike ordinary Sunshard Crystals.
- He asks the party about the events beneath the Royal Plaza during Session 1.
- When the players explain that the original crystal was once whole, he becomes visibly concerned.
- He recalls old accounts describing a powerful crystal associated with maintaining the balance of the Sundered Isles.
- He theorizes that the strange phenomena are connected to the shattering of the crystal.
- He believes recovering and reuniting the scattered fragments could prevent further instability.

**DM Secret:** The archivist is not revealing everything he knows. His close relationship with King Elon may influence the information he shares.

### Descriptions / Atmosphere:

> Sir Pugsington leads you through the grand halls of the Solcrest Library and into a cluttered workshop.
> 
> Books, ancient artifacts, and mechanical components cover nearly every available surface.
> 
> Behind a large workbench stands a humanoid construct, its body assembled from aged metal, stone, and carefully repaired mechanical parts.
> 
> Its glowing eyes turn toward you as Pugsington approaches.
> 
> "You have brought visitors, Sir Pugsington. I assume this is not a social occasion."
> 
> Pugsington places the recovered crystal fragment upon the workbench.
> 
> The construct examines it in silence.
> 
> "Where did you find this?"

### NPCs / Enemies:

- Sir Pugsington
- Ancient Solcrest Archivist (Name TBD)

### Player Choices / Alternate Outcomes:

**Arcana DC 13:** Assist the archivist in examining the fragment and identify similarities to the crystal beneath the Royal Plaza.

**History DC 14:** Recall obscure references to ancient magical disasters or legends surrounding the Sundering.

**Insight DC 13:** Notice that the archivist becomes uncomfortable when discussing ancient royal records.

**Persuasion DC 14:** Convince the archivist to share an additional historical account or obscure prophecy.

**Investigation DC 13:** Notice that several books and records concerning the Sundering are stored separately from the rest of the library's collection.

**Important Information:**

The archivist may explain:

- The fragment contains an enormous amount of unfamiliar magical energy.
- Its properties differ from ordinary Sunshard Crystals.
- Ancient records contain vague references to a powerful crystal associated with the world's stability.
- The original crystal's destruction may be responsible for the growing instability across the Isles.
- Reuniting the fragments could potentially restore balance.

**Important:** The archivist does not confirm that reuniting the crystal will save the world. He presents it as his working theory.
