## 🗺️ Travel Speed

**1 map grid square = 1 standard travel day on foot.**

|Travel Tier|Squares / Day|Examples|
|---|---|---|
|🚶 Standard|1|Foot travel, heavily loaded party|
|🐎 Swift|2|Horses, mounts, wagons, regional transportation|
|⚡ Rapid|3|Ships, airships, exceptional mounts, magical vehicles|

> **Regional Rule:** The mechanics remain the same, but available transportation changes by continent. A Swift journey through Ilarya may look completely different from one through the Iron Hills.

## ⏳ Journey Length & Events

|Total Travel Time|Journey Events|
|---|---|
|1–3 days|1|
|4–6 days|2|
|7–9 days|3|
|10–12 days|4|
|13–15 days|5|
|16–18 days|6|
|19–21 days|7|
|+3 days|+1 event|

### Quick Rule

> **Journey Events = Travel Days ÷ 3, rounded up.**

These events are distributed throughout the journey rather than necessarily occurring every three days exactly.

# 🎒 Supplies & Exhaustion

Normal travel **does not cause exhaustion by itself** as long as the party is adequately supplied and resting.

|Travel Condition|Effect|
|---|---|
|🟢 Properly Supplied|No exhaustion checks|
|🟡 Low Supplies|CON save may be required|
|🔴 No Food / Water|Exhaustion rules apply|
|🏃 Forced Travel|CON save against exhaustion|
|🌡️ Extreme Environment|Equipment/supplies may negate or reduce checks|
|🏕️ Safe Settlement|Rest, resupply and recovery available|
|🌧️ Poor Camp Conditions|May prevent full travel recovery at DM discretion|

### Suggested Supply Rule

Rather than tracking twenty-seven individual tent pegs:

**1 Travel Ration = food + basic consumable supplies for one character for one day.**

Water can usually be replenished naturally unless the region specifically makes it important.

This lets survival-focused regions change the rules. Crossing a desert, volcanic wasteland, frozen mountain range, etc. can require **Regional Supplies** in addition to normal rations.

# 🎭 Event Importance

After determining the category, you can optionally roll **1d6** to determine how significant it is.

|d6|Significance|Meaning|
|---|---|---|
|1–2|🟢 Minor|Flavor, brief interaction, small discovery|
|3–4|🟡 Notable|Meaningful encounter, reward, information or danger|
|5|🟠 Major|Possible side quest, significant threat or discovery|
|6|🔴 Exceptional|Rare event that could affect the campaign|

This gives you a nice little two-dice engine:

**d20 = WHAT happens.**  
**d6 = HOW IMPORTANT it is.**

So a **5 + 1** might be harmless glowing crystal butterflies.

A **5 + 6** might be...

_"Guys, why is the mountain moving?"_

💎😬

# 🏘️ Route Locations

Locations directly along the party's planned route generate **opportunities in addition to random Journey Events.**

|Location|Travel Effect|
|---|---|
|🏘️ Village|Rest + supplies + optional side quest|
|🏙️ Town|Safe rest + supplies + services + quests|
|👑 City|Major services + transportation + factions + quests|
|🏚️ Known Ruin|Optional exploration|
|🛕 Shrine|Religious/social interaction|
|🏰 Fort|Faction encounter / safety / danger|
|⚓ Port|Access to regional transportation|
|❓ Landmark|Optional discovery or encounter|

### Village Rule

> **Every village has at least one optional adventure hook.**

Players never have to stop simply because their route crosses the location.

# 📖 Between-Session Travel

If the journey occurs between sessions, **don't play the encounters normally.**

Roll on a separate narrative table and weave the results into the next recap.

## 🎲 Narrative Journey Table

|d20|Travel Sequence|
|---|---|
|1|Severe weather follows the party for part of the journey|
|2|The party passes evidence of a recent battle|
|3|Something watches the party from a distance|
|4|A strange creature briefly follows them|
|5|They encounter travelers heading the opposite direction|
|6|Someone shares a strange local rumor|
|7|They discover the remains of an abandoned campsite|
|8|An unusually peaceful night gives everyone time to talk|
|9|They witness a beautiful regional natural phenomenon|
|10|A harmless magical phenomenon occurs|
|11|They encounter a merchant or caravan|
|12|They pass a shrine, memorial or religious procession|
|13|Something strange happens to one of their possessions|
|14|They discover tracks belonging to something enormous|
|15|They hear or witness distant crystal activity|
|16|A traveler tells them news from their destination|
|17|They discover an unusual object along the road|
|18|Something inexplicable occurs during the night|
|19|They meet a memorable friendly traveler|
|**20**|They witness something they will remember for the rest of their lives|

The clever bit is that **these don't have to disappear after the recap**.

If you roll **17**, you could tell them:

> _During your journey, you found a weathered silver key beside an abandoned milestone._

And then actually give them:

**🔑 Mysterious Silver Key**

They have absolutely no idea what it opens.

Welcome to DM gardening. Plant nonsense now, decide what horrifying tree grows from it later. 🌱
