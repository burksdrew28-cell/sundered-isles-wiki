_Side Quest / Rumor_

A completely ordinary goose has become a minor menace in [[Solcrest]]. It has been stealing food, chasing townsfolk, and biting anyone foolish enough to corner it. The brass key around its neck has convinced several locals that something mysterious is going on.

There is not.

The goose is simply an asshole.

---

## 📋 Quest Info

- **Status:** Available
    
- **Location:** [[Solcrest]]
    
- **Main Objective:** Catch the goose and recover the brass key.
    
- **Reward:** 8 SP and whatever the goose has stolen.
    

---

## 🎣 Hook

The party hears locals complaining about a goose that has been terrorizing the neighborhood.

If they investigate, they quickly spot it causing another incident nearby.

---

## 🧭 Main Beats

1. **Find the Goose** — It is stealing food from a merchant or chasing someone through the street.
    
2. **The Chase** — The goose bolts through alleys, market stalls, fountains, carts, and pedestrians.
    
3. **Catch the Goose** — The party eventually corners, tackles, charms, feeds, or otherwise defeats their feathered adversary.
    

---

## 🎲 Chase Ideas

Use whatever checks fit the players' ideas.

- **Athletics** — Keep pace or tackle it.
    
- **Acrobatics** — Leap obstacles during the chase.
    
- **Animal Handling** — Calm or lure it.
    
- **Sleight of Hand** — Snatch the key.
    
- **Persuasion** — Does not work. It is a goose.
    

Failed checks should cause funny complications rather than ending the chase.

---

## 💰 Rewards

The goose is carrying:

- A small brass key
    
- 3 CP
    
- A button
    
- Half a bread roll
    
- Something shiny it absolutely should not have
    

The brass key opens an abandoned garden shed nearby containing nothing particularly valuable.

---

## 🔀 Outcome

**Success:** The neighborhood celebrates the party's victory over the goose.

**Failure / Ignored:** The goose continues its reign of terror and may randomly appear again later.

---

## 🧠 DM Notes

- The goose has no magical abilities.
    
- There is no conspiracy.
    
- The key is not important.
    
- Play the goose with absolute confidence and zero fear.
    
- If the party becomes convinced there is deeper lore, never confirm or deny anything.
