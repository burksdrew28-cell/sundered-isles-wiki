# 🎲 Session 1 - The Royal Wedding

> [!summary] SESSION FLOW  
> **Arrival → Crowd → Gate → Pugsington → Explore Solcrest → Royal Wedding → Earthquake → Disaster → Plaza Collapse → Vault → Fey Goddess → Escape → Crystal Meteor Shower**

---

# 🧭 Quick DM Reminders

- Ask **"What do you do?"** before calling for checks.
    
- Failure should create complications, **not stop the story**.
    
- Reward creative spells/abilities.
    
- Let the players improvise and explore.
    
- Don't over-explain ancient lore yet.
    
- The beginning should feel **fun and safe**.
    
- The ending should feel **massive and mysterious**.
    

---

# 🏰 PHASE 1 - ARRIVAL & THE GATE

## Opening Description

The characters arrive separately outside [[Solcrest]] for [[Princess Seleni]]'s royal wedding.

Outside the gates:

- Massive crowd
    
- Peasants, travelers, merchants & nobles
    
- Animals and wagons everywhere
    
- Blue/gold wedding decorations
    
- Vendors working the crowd
    
- Guards desperately managing entry
    

### Character Introductions

Ask each player:

**"What does the crowd see as your character approaches Solcrest?"**

Let them describe their character.

---

## 👥 Get Through the Crowd

Ask:

**"How are you trying to reach the gate?"**

### Common Checks

|Approach|Check|
|---|---|
|Push through|Athletics|
|Squeeze through|Acrobatics|
|Find an easier route|Investigation|
|Watch crowd movement|Perception|
|Talk people aside|Persuasion|
|Pretend to be important|Deception|
|Scare people aside|Intimidation|

**DC 11-13**

### Failed Check

They still progress.

Use a quick complication:

- Food/drink spilled
    
- Goat grabs something
    
- Pickpocket attempts 1d4 SP
    
- Sent the wrong direction
    
- Run into another player
    

> [!tip]  
> Use players bumping into each other to naturally begin bringing the characters together.

# 🚪 PARTY MEETS AT THE GATE

Eventually all five end up together near the front.

Say:

> "After fighting your way through the crowd, all five of you eventually find yourselves squeezed into the same small pocket of space near the gate."

Ask:

**"What's the first thing your character notices about one of the others?"**

Go around once.

Then the guard calls:

> **"Next group! Names, purpose of visit, and proof of entry."**

If they say they aren't together:

> **"Wonderful. You are now. I've got six hundred people behind you. Purpose of visit?"**

---

# 🛡️ The Guard Refuses Entry

The city is at maximum capacity because of the wedding.

The guard cannot allow more visitors inside.

Let the party attempt solutions first.

### Useful Checks

**Persuasion DC 13**  
Guard sympathizes but cannot override orders.

**Insight DC 11**  
He's exhausted, not cruel.

**Perception DC 12**  
People carrying royal seals are being allowed through.

**Deception DC 14-15**  
Fake wedding staff, entertainers, nobility, etc.

Allow:

- Invitations
    
- Bribery
    
- Lying
    
- Sneaking
    
- Creative ideas
    

None should completely solve it.

---

# 🐾 ENTER SIR PUGSINGTON

When the gate scene has had enough time:

Guards suddenly straighten and salute.

[[Sir Pugsington]] arrives in royal armor.

Guard:

> **"Sir Pugsington."**

Pugsington looks at the party.

> **"They're with me."**

Guard:

> "All of them?"

> **"...Apparently."**

# 💍 The Ceremony

KEEP THIS SHORT.

## 🌸 Welcome

**Officiant:**

> “People of Solcrest, friends from across Astra Veyra, honored guests, and those of you who somehow found a place to stand... welcome.”

> “Today we gather not merely to witness the joining of two lives, but to celebrate the beginning of a new chapter for this family, this kingdom, and, if the kitchen staff have done their jobs correctly, an exceptionally large feast.”

---

## ✨ Blessing

**Officiant:**

> “May your home know laughter more often than silence, patience more often than anger, and love even on the days when one of you is unquestionably, spectacularly wrong.”

> “May the road ahead be long, your burdens shared, your joys multiplied, and may neither of you ever discover exactly how much this wedding cost.”

---

## 💙 Short Vows

**Princess Seleni:**

> “I promise to stand beside you when the road is easy, when it is difficult, and when we have absolutely no idea what we are doing.”

> “I promise to choose you in every season of our lives, even when you steal the blankets.”

**Her Partner:**

> “I promise to protect your heart, encourage your dreams, and remind you when you've forgotten to eat because you've become far too busy taking care of everyone else.”

> “And I promise that should we ever argue over the blankets, I will accept that history will remember you as the victor.”

---

## 💍 Rings

**Officiant:**

> “A ring has no beginning and no end, which makes it a fitting symbol for love... and a remarkably inconvenient thing to drop in front of several thousand people.”

_Pause while the rings are exchanged._

> “Place these rings upon one another's hands as a promise freely made and faithfully kept.”

---

## 👑 Declaration of Marriage

**Officiant:**

> “Before your families, your kingdom, and approximately half the population of Solcrest, you have made your promises to one another.”

> “By the authority entrusted to me by the Crown, the gods, and a truly alarming amount of ceremonial paperwork... I declare you married.”

---

## 🎉 Cheers

**Officiant:**

> “People of Solcrest, it is my great honor to present your newly married royal couple!”

**Crowd:**

> “LONG LIVE PRINCESS SELENI!”

> “LONG LIVE THE ROYAL HOUSE!”

Then let the bells erupt, flower petals fly, music begin, and the crowd absolutely lose its mind.

For one extra little laugh, somewhere during the cheering a child can loudly yell:

> **“NOW DO THEY KISS?!”**

And someone nearby immediately goes:

> **“YES, THAT IS GENERALLY HOW THIS WORKS.”**

### Fountain Tradition

The newlyweds approach the central fountain.

Each places a flower into the water.

The officiant says:

> **"As the waters of Solcrest carry ever onward, so too may your lives move together."**

Crowd cheers.

# 💎 PHASE 5 - THE VAULT

## Awakening

Darkness.

Dust.

Ringing ears.

Dripping water.

Ask:

**"What's the first thing your character does?"**

They awaken on the **central platform** of an enormous ancient underground chamber.

---

# 🕯️ Vault Reveal

Describe:

- Huge black body of water
    
- Ancient bridges/platforms
    
- Massive stone architecture
    
- Crystals throughout
    
- Three empty fountains
    
- Upper platforms
    
- Broken statues
    
- No obvious exit
    
- Something occasionally moving in the water
    

### Checks

**Perception DC 10**  
Hear shifting stone and distant movement.

**Investigation DC 11**  
This was never designed as an entrance.

**History DC 13**  
Architecture is far older than modern Solcrest.

**Arcana DC 12**  
Crystals are magical.

**Nature DC 13**  
Something large is moving beneath the water.

---

# 🐀 THE GIANT RAT

Introduce the rat early.

It watches them.

It does not attack.

If approached, it retreats.

If fed, it accepts food.

If someone uses Speak with Animals:

> **"Bad place."**

"Why?"

> **"Bad."**

"What's in the water?"

> **"...Worse."**

Keep track of how the party treats it.

---

# 💎 THE THREE FOUNTAINS PUZZLE

Three empty fountains are spread throughout the vault.

There are **12 loose crystals** hidden around the chamber:

- 4 Pink
    
- 4 Purple
    
- 4 Blue
    

Each fountain is linked to one crystal color and requires **4 matching crystals** to fully activate.

### Investigation DC 10

Notice four crystal-shaped slots built into the fountain.

### Arcana DC 12

The fountain appears to channel magical energy into something elsewhere in the vault.

When a correct crystal is inserted:

- Crystal locks into place
    
- Part of the fountain begins glowing
    
- Ancient markings activate
    
- The fountain reacts more strongly with each crystal added
    

When all **4 matching crystals** are placed:

- Fountain fully activates
    
- Water begins flowing
    
- Ancient markings flare with light
    
- A linked bridge rises into place over the water
    

**4 Matching Crystals → Fountain Activated → Bridge Raised**

Repeat for all three colors.

---

- Bridges

The Maw is an environmental threat, NOT something they should casually fight.

Encourage:

- Ropes
    
- Spells
    
- Helping allies
    
- Getting out of the water FAST
    

---

# 🪨 THE STONE GOLEM BRIDGE

As they travel along the left-side bridges toward the upper platform:

An enormous stone statue stands dormant.

### Perception DC 14

Notice unusual cracking around its feet.

Then:

Its eye lights.

**THOOM.**

The golem steps.

The bridge collapses beneath part of the group.

---

## Bridge Collapse

Anyone on collapsing section:

### DEX Save DC 13

**Success:** Reach stable ground.

**Failure:** Begin falling.

Then:

### Athletics / Acrobatics DC 12

Grab onto something.

Other players may use reactions/spells creatively.

If they fall completely:

**They hit the water.**

Cue Maw panic.

---

# 🪨 THE GOLEM

The golem is primarily an **environmental guardian**.

It can:

- Smash bridges
    
- Block routes
    
- Force movement
    
- Create falling debris
    

Players CAN fight it, but escaping/getting around it is usually smarter.

# 🏆 THE TREASURE ROOM

# ✨ FEY GODDESS REVEAL

The giant rat walks into the room.

Stops.

Begins glowing.

Its form changes.

The rat becomes the trapped **Fey Goddess**.

She remembers everything the party did to her.

Possible callbacks:

If fed:

> **"Your cheese was appreciated."**

If insulted:

> **"You called me an oversized sewer potato."**

If attacked:

She simply looks at them.

> **"You."**
