# 💎 THE THREE FOUNTAINS PUZZLE

Three empty fountains are spread throughout the vault.

There are **12 loose crystals** hidden around the chamber:

- 4 Pink
    
- 4 Purple
    
- 4 Blue
    

Each fountain is linked to one crystal color and requires **4 matching crystals** to fully activate.

### Investigation DC 10

Notice four crystal-shaped slots built into the fountain.

### Arcana DC 12

The fountain appears to channel magical energy into something elsewhere in the vault.

When a correct crystal is inserted:

- Crystal locks into place
    
- Part of the fountain begins glowing
    
- Ancient markings activate
    
- The fountain reacts more strongly with each crystal added
    

When all **4 matching crystals** are placed:

- Fountain fully activates
    
- Water begins flowing
    
- Ancient markings flare with light
    
- A linked bridge rises into place over the water
    

**4 Matching Crystals → Fountain Activated → Bridge Raised**

Repeat for all three colors.

## Bridge Collapse

Anyone on collapsing section:

### DEX Save DC 13

**Success:** Reach stable ground.

**Failure:** Begin falling.

Then:

### Athletics / Acrobatics DC 12

Grab onto something.

Other players may use reactions/spells creatively.

If they fall completely:

**They hit the water.**

Cue Maw panic.

# 🔮 Simple Vision System

When a player touches the big crystal, have them make a check.

## Option A — easiest

Use a **DC 12 Wisdom save** for everyone.

## Option B — a little more flavorful

Let them use a class-themed skill:

- **Fighter:** History or Wisdom
- **Ranger:** Survival or Nature
- **Druid:** Nature
- **Cleric:** Religion
- **Bard:** History or Performance

If you want it super simple for the table, I’d honestly do:

> **DC 12 class-themed check**  
> or  
> **Wisdom if nothing fits**

---

# 🌟 Result Tiers

## Failure

They get only a **fragment**:

- emotion
- flashes
- one strong image
- no context

## Success

They get:

- a short clear scene
- one ancient figure’s perspective
- one clue about the Sundering

## Great Success

If they beat the DC by **5 or more**, or roll a **nat 20**:

- they get a sharper version
- one extra detail
- maybe a short sentence they hear

That gives you three clean levels without making it complicated.

# 🕰️ Core Theme of the Visions

All the visions should feel like they come from the **same ancient crisis**:

- the world is in danger
- the crystals matter
- people are trying to stop something
- something is being **sealed / shattered / broken / sacrificed**
- nobody fully understands it in the present day

So every class gets a different angle on the same old catastrophe.

---

# ⚔️ Fighter Vision

## Fail

You see a flash of gold and stone.  
A shield rises in trembling hands.  
The ground splits.  
People are screaming.  
Then you see a single enormous crystal glowing so brightly it hurts to look at.  
The vision ends with one overwhelming feeling:

> **Hold the line.**

## Success

For a moment, you are someone else.

You stand on a crumbling wall in ancient armor, shield raised, dust and burning light filling the air. Around you, defenders brace against something unseen beyond a blinding fracture in the sky. Below, civilians flee toward a massive crystal structure as bells ring wildly.

A commander near you shouts:

> “Buy them time!”

You do not know who “them” are — only that whatever is happening is bigger than war.

## Great Success

Add:

You glance down and see a crest on your armor you don’t recognize — a kingdom or order long forgotten. As the crystal pulses, every soldier around you drops to one knee for just a moment, like the force of its power is crushing the air itself.

---

# 🏹 Ranger Vision

## Fail

You see treetops whipping in violent wind.  
Birds explode from the branches in panic.  
The earth shakes beneath your feet.  
In the distance, a line of white light tears across the horizon.

You feel:

> **Something natural is being wounded.**

## Success

You are running through an ancient forest at dusk, bow in hand. The animals are fleeing in every direction — deer, foxes, birds, even predators running side by side. You crest a ridge and see the land ahead split by glowing crystal veins pulsing under the earth like lightning trapped in roots.

Another ranger beside you whispers:

> “It’s spreading too fast.”

Far off, towers and stone circles shine with the same terrible light.

## Great Success

Add:

You spot figures in the distance guiding people away from danger, but they keep looking back toward a single blinding source — as if the whole disaster began in one place.

---

# 🌿 Druid Vision

## Fail

You smell rain, soil, and sap.  
A great tree groans like it is in pain.  
Roots crack.  
Water trembles.  
Then a crystal pulse ripples through the land, and every leaf around you turns toward it.

You feel:

> **The world is trying to survive something unnatural.**

## Success

You stand barefoot in a sacred grove, hands pressed to the roots of an enormous ancient tree. Druids in old robes form a circle around a crystal embedded in the earth. The rivers nearby churn, the wind screams through the branches, and the very ground feels alive with panic.

One elder says quietly:

> “If it breaks, everything breaks.”

Then the light surges so hard the vision nearly tears away from you.

## Great Success

Add:

For a moment, you sense the land itself crying out — not in words, but in fear. You understand that the crystals are not just objects. They are connected to something vast and living.

---

# ✨ Cleric Vision

## Fail

You hear chanting.  
A candle flame blows sideways even though there is no wind.  
Cracks race through a stone floor toward an altar.  
A crystal above it glows like a second sun.

You feel:

> **prayer mixed with terror**

## Success

You are inside a great ancient temple. Priests kneel in rows while beams of pale light stream through shattered windows. At the center of the chamber, a large crystal hangs suspended above an altar, flickering violently. Every prayer in the room sounds less like worship and more like desperation.

A priest beside you says:

> “The gods hear us… but they are far away.”

Then the crystal flashes, and every voice stops at once.

## Great Success

Add:

You briefly see holy symbols from many faiths together in the same place, as if old enemies or strangers had united for this one terrible moment.

---

# 🎵 Bard Vision

## Fail

You hear music — or what used to be music.  
Strings snap.  
A voice breaks mid-song.  
Then a strange humming note rises from a crystal and drowns out everything else.

You feel:

> **the memory of something beautiful being lost**

## Success

You stand in a vast ancient hall full of scribes, musicians, and nobles. The room is shaking, but one bard continues singing beside a glowing crystal pedestal, as if trying to preserve a final story before disaster swallows it. Scrolls spill to the floor. Ink runs from overturned jars. No one is celebrating — they are recording.

Someone says:

> “If we survive, they must remember.”

Then the hall trembles so violently the scene shatters.

## Great Success

Add:

You catch one extra detail: the song is not a triumph. It is a lament — a farewell to a world that is already changing.

# 💎 THE CRYSTAL RISES

Then...

### 🔮 The Collective Vision

The crystal erupts above you in a blinding flash.

For a moment, there is no sound.

No falling stone.  
No screaming.  
No wind.

Then the world disappears.

All five of you stand together in an endless black void.

Far ahead, something begins to move.

A vast cloud of darkness stretches across the horizon, larger than any storm you have ever seen. Within it, two enormous **purple eyes** slowly open.

They look directly at you.

You cannot tell whether the presence is a creature, a god, or something far older.

Then a voice fills the void.

Not loud.

Not angry.

Almost relieved.

> **“At last…”**

The darkness shifts.

> **“You have my gratitude.”**

The eyes narrow slightly.

> **“For what you have done.”**

And then...

You are back.

A strange glow appears beneath the destroyed plaza.

The same crystal energy from the vault.

The ground begins vibrating.

A brilliant crystal erupts from beneath Solcrest.

It shoots:

**UP.**

Past the castle.

Past the clouds.

Higher.

Higher.

Until it becomes a tiny point of light.

Everyone watches.

Then...

# CRACK.

The crystal appears to strike **the sky itself**.

A massive flash spreads across the heavens.

For only a moment, strange magical lines become visible across the sky.

The characters don't know what they are seeing.

Then:

# THE CRYSTAL SHATTERS.
