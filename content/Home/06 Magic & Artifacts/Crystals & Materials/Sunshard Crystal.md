_Crystal & Material · Uncommon_

A naturally occurring golden crystal found in several regions of [[Astra Veyra]]. Sunshards range from tiny translucent fragments to larger crystalline formations and are prized for the warm glow they produce when struck by direct sunlight.

---

### ✨ Known Properties

Sunshard Crystals can hold small amounts of magical energy and are frequently used in jewelry, decorative objects, magical tools, and simple enchantments.

### 📖 Lore

Sunshards have been collected and traded throughout Astra Veyra for centuries. While valuable, they are common enough that most people are familiar with them, especially in larger settlements such as [[Solcrest]].
