*Weapon · Uncommon*

![[Shattershardaxe.png]]

A throwing axe forged from pale azure crystal, with a faceted edge that catches the light like a prism. Its dark leather-wrapped haft is set with tiny crystal fragments that glow faintly in dim conditions.

---

### ✨ Known Properties

The Shattershard Throwing Axe is a magical weapon that grants a **+1 bonus to attack and damage rolls**.

When thrown, the axe magically returns to the wielder’s hand immediately after the attack, whether it hits or misses.

As a bonus action, its command word can be spoken to make the blade shed bright light for 10 feet and dim light for another 10 feet.

**Damage:** 1d6 slashing  
**Range:** 20/60 ft.  
**Versatile:** 1d8  
**Attunement:** Required by a creature proficient with martial weapons

### 📖 Lore

Shattershard axes were forged by dwarven smiths from rare veins of living crystal found deep beneath the mountains.

Only a limited number were ever created, and over the centuries many disappeared from dwarven vaults. Surviving axes occasionally resurface in the hands of adventurers, collectors, and warriors.
