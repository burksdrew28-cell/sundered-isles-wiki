_Magical Phenomenon · Common_

Soft blue, gold, and occasionally silver lights that can sometimes be seen drifting above the [[Provon River]] after sunset. They move slowly with the current, often gathering in small clusters before fading away.

---

### ✨ Known Properties

Riverlights are harmless and usually last only a few minutes. They cannot normally be touched or captured and seem to appear most often on clear, calm nights.

### 📖 Lore

The lights have been seen along the Provon River for generations. Locals have countless stories about them, with some believing they bring good fortune while others simply regard them as one of the river's natural magical quirks.
