_Artifact · Royal Regalia_

The ceremonial crown worn by the reigning monarch of the [[Kingdom of Solcrest]]. Crafted from polished gold and decorated with gemstones, its distinctive sunburst design has become one of the most recognizable symbols of the kingdom.

---

### ✨ Known Properties

The Crown of Solcrest is primarily a symbol of royal authority and is traditionally worn during coronations, royal ceremonies, and other major events.

### 📖 Lore

The crown has been passed down through the royal family for generations and is closely associated with the history of Solcrest. Images of the crown appear throughout the capital on banners, statues, coins, and official seals.
