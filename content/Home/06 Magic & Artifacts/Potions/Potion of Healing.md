_Potion · Common_

A small vial of vivid red liquid commonly carried by adventurers, guards, healers, and travelers throughout the [[Sundered Isles]]. The potion has a faint herbal scent and a slightly sweet, metallic taste.

---

### ✨ Known Properties

Drinking a Potion of Healing restores a small amount of health and helps close minor wounds, ease pain, and stabilize injuries.

**Effect:** Regain **2d4 + 2 Hit Points** when consumed.

### 📖 Lore

Healing potions are among the most common magical remedies in the Sundered Isles. They can often be purchased from apothecaries, alchemists, temples, and well-stocked general stores, though their price can make them a valuable resource for ordinary travelers.
