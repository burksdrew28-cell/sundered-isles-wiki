# Amulet of Shattered Reflections

*Magical Item · Very Rare*

![[Amulet.png]]

A finely crafted amulet set with a large, multifaceted crystal suspended in delicate silver and mithral filigree. Light catches within the crystal and breaks into shifting prisms across nearby surfaces. When held close, the crystal feels cool and gives off a faint hum.

---

### ✨ Known Properties

The Amulet of Shattered Reflections has **3 charges** and regains **1d3 expended charges each dawn**.

While wearing the amulet, you can use an action to expend 1 charge and activate one of the following effects:

- **Prism Step:** Teleport to an unoccupied space you can see within **15 feet**. If you end the teleportation in dim light or darkness, you gain advantage on your next saving throw before the end of your next turn.

- **Shard Barrier:** Create a **5-foot-square crystalline barrier** on a surface you can see within **30 feet**. It lasts for 1 minute or until it absorbs **20 damage**. The barrier has **AC 15**, is immune to poison and psychic damage, and provides three-quarters cover to creatures behind it.

- **Fragment Lens:** For **1 minute**, you gain the benefits of *See Invisibility* for yourself and can also see through nonmagical fog, smoke, and similar lightly obscuring effects.

### 📖 Lore

Legends claim the first Amulet of Shattered Reflections was created by a secluded crystal sage fascinated by light, illusion, and perception.

Its unusual abilities made similar amulets highly sought after by explorers, spies, and treasure hunters, who prized them for their combination of mobility, protection, and enhanced sight.
