Most merchants throughout the **Sundered Isles** recognize standard coinage. Some regions, organizations, and particularly unusual merchants may also accept other valuables.

## 🪙 Standard Currency

|Currency|Abbreviation|Value|
|---|---|---|
|🟤 Copper Piece|**CP**|1 CP|
|⚪ Silver Piece|**SP**|10 CP|
|🟡 Electrum Piece|**EP**|5 SP|
|🟨 Gold Piece|**GP**|10 SP|
|💠 Platinum Piece|**PP**|10 GP|

### Quick Conversion

||CP|SP|GP|PP|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**1 SP**|10|1|0.1|0.01|
|**1 GP**|100|10|1|0.1|
|**1 PP**|1,000|100|10|1|

> **Easy Rule:**  
> **10 Copper = 1 Silver**  
> **10 Silver = 1 Gold**  
> **10 Gold = 1 Platinum**

## ☀️ Sunshard Crystals

[[Sunshard Crystal]]s are naturally occurring magical crystals found throughout the [[Sundered Isles]]. Though not officially minted currency, their rarity and usefulness make them valuable almost everywhere.

|Type|Typical Value|
|---|---|
|✨ **Sunshard Fragment**|~5 GP|
|☀️ **Sunshard Crystal**|~50 GP|
|🌟 **Large / Exceptional Sunshard**|Value varies|

Their exact value can change depending on **size, clarity, magical potency, and location**.

Some merchants may even prefer Sunshards over coin.

## 📜 Exchange Notes

- Most everyday purchases use **Copper, Silver, and Gold**.
- **Platinum** is uncommon outside wealthy merchants, nobles, banks, and major cities.
- **Electrum** exists, but many merchants find it annoying.
- Valuable objects can sometimes be **bartered directly**.
- Currency and exchange rates may vary between regions.
- Strange currencies discovered during adventures will be added here as they become known.
