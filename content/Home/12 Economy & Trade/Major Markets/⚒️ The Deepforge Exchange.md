**Location:** [[Black Forge]], [[Iornveil]]  
**Favored Currency:** Gold Pieces, Platinum Pieces, Precious Metals & Gemstones

The **Deepforge Exchange** is the beating mercantile heart of [[Black Forge]], carved into vast stone halls beneath the dwarven capital.

Its stalls and vault-front shops deal in **rare ores, refined metals, masterwork armor, legendary weapons, ancient artifacts, gemstones, and relics recovered from forgotten dwarven holds**.

Everything here carries weight, often literally. Merchants value craftsmanship, provenance, and material quality above flashy appearances, and a well-forged blade may command more respect than a purse overflowing with coin.

The Exchange is also one of the best-known places in the Sundered Isles to find unusual metals or weapons with histories stretching back centuries.

Counterfeits are rare.

Mostly because trying to sell one to a dwarven smith is considered an excellent way to become part of the floor. ⚒️🪙
