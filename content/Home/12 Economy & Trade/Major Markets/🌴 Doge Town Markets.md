**Location:** [[Doge Town]], [[Ilyara]]  
**Favored Currency:** Gold Pieces, Silver Pieces & Pirate Doubloons

Spread throughout the warm streets, docks, and waterfront plazas of Doge Town, these tropical markets are loud, colorful, and rarely quiet for more than a few seconds.

Island fruits, seafood, carved trinkets, imported goods, strange spices, shells, jewelry, nautical equipment, and questionable curiosities are all common sights. Many stalls are little more than canvas shades tied between palm trees, while others operate directly from boats pulled alongside the docks.

Haggling is practically considered a local sport.
