**Location:** [[Canalaton]], [[Velora]]  
**Favored Currency:** Gold & Silver Pieces

The Canal Market stretches along [[Canalaton]]'s waterways, bridges, docks, and narrow streets.

Boats arrive constantly carrying **fish, food, spices, produce, cargo, and trade goods from across [[The Sundered Isles]]**, making it one of the best places to encounter goods from distant regions without traveling there yourself.

Much of the market operates directly from barges and floating stalls, creating a constantly shifting maze of merchants, fishermen, cooks, sailors, and customers along the canals.
