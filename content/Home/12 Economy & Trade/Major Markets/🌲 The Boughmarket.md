**Location:** [[Barlop]], [[Eladrion]]  
**Favored Currency:** Silver Pieces, Gold Pieces & Barter

Built beneath the enormous trees surrounding [[Barlop]], the **Boughmarket** feels less like a marketplace and more like a small settlement woven directly into the forest.

Wooden stalls, hanging lanterns, rope bridges, canvas shelters, and carved platforms cluster beneath the branches. Hunters, herbalists, woodworkers, travelers, farmers, and traders from the nearby [[Refu Forest]] frequently pass through.

The market is particularly known for **forest goods, herbs, timbercraft, preserved foods, traveling supplies, and locally gathered materials**.

Trade and barter are common, especially among locals who may value a useful tool, rare herb, or good bottle of something more than a handful of coins.
