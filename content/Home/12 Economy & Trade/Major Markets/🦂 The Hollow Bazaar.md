**Location:** Unknown  
**Favored Currency:** Gold Pieces, Sunshard Crystals & Barter

Few people can agree on exactly where the **Hollow Bazaar** is located.

Travelers speak of a sprawling desert marketplace hidden somewhere beneath the sands, accessible through caves, forgotten ruins, or secret entrances known only to particular traders.

The Bazaar supposedly deals in rare goods, unusual artifacts, forbidden merchandise, information, and anything else that respectable merchants might suddenly become very interested in not knowing about.

Whether the Hollow Bazaar actually exists is another matter entirely.
