**Location:** [[Solcrest]], [[Astra Veyra]]  
**Favored Currency:** Gold & Silver Pieces

The primary public market of [[Solcrest]], filled with farmers, craftsmen, merchants, and townsfolk going about their daily business. It is the best place in the capital to find **necessities, local produce, simple equipment, household goods, and wares made throughout the surrounding countryside**.

The market is busy but orderly, with colorful awnings, packed stalls, street food, wagons, and the occasional merchant loudly insisting that his turnips are somehow superior to every other turnip in the kingdom.

# Solcrest Market Stalls
