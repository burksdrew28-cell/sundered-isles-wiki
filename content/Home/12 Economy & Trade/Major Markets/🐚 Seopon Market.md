**Location:** [[Seopon]], [[Ilyara]]
**Favored Currency:** Platinum Pieces & Pearls

The **[[Seopon]] Market** is one of the most luxurious marketplaces in the Sundered Isles, set among warm tropical streets, polished stone promenades, shaded courtyards, and breezy waterfront terraces.

Unlike the louder island markets, [[Seopon]] is refined, curated, and unapologetically expensive. Merchants specialize in **rare jewelry, exotic fabrics, fine art, unusual magical curiosities, imported delicacies, ornate weapons, and one-of-a-kind treasures** gathered from distant shores.

Pearls are especially prized here and are often traded directly alongside platinum coin.

Many stalls resemble private galleries more than shops, and discovering something truly strange among the polished displays is almost expected.

In Seopon, the question usually isn't whether something can be found.

It's whether you can afford it. 🐚💎
