The **Solcrest Crownward** is the primary military force of [[The Crown of Astra Veyra]], charged with defending the capital, protecting Astra Veyra's borders, maintaining the kingdom's forts, and answering threats throughout the realm. Their white-and-gold standards are a familiar sight along the roads surrounding Solcrest, and most citizens regard the Crownward as disciplined, dependable, and fiercely loyal to the Crown.

Though centered in [[Solcrest]], Crownward soldiers are stationed throughout [[Astra Veyra]], particularly along important roads, settlements, forts, and areas considered vulnerable to raiders or other dangers.
