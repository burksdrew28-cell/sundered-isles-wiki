The **[[The Crown of Astra Veyra]]** is one of the most influential powers within [[Astra Veyra]], ruled from the prosperous capital city of [[Solcrest]]. Known for its wealth, fertile lands, strong royal traditions, and thriving trade, the kingdom has remained remarkably stable for generations. To most of its citizens, the Solcrest monarchy represents order, prosperity, and protection in an otherwise unpredictable world.
