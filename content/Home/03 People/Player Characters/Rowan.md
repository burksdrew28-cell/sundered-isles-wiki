*Human • **Cleric***

Rowan Emberstone is a kind and curious cleric of [[Lathander]] who spent nearly her entire life beneath the mountains of [[Iornveil]]. Raised around a small temple in [[Black Forge]], she learned early that even a little light can make an enormous difference in dark places. Fiercely protective of others and eager to experience the world beyond her home, Rowan approaches adventure with equal parts faith and wonder.

---

### 🧭 Basic Information

- **Homeland:** [[Black Forge]], [[Iornveil]]
- **Age:** 28
- **Ideals:** Light should be used to guide, protect, and help those in need.
- **Affiliation:** Faith of [[Lathander]]

### 👤 Appearance

Rowan is a 5'4" human woman with pale skin, blonde hair, and soft light-green eyes.

Her clothing and equipment reflect both her life as an acolyte and her role as a traveling cleric. She carries herself with a welcoming warmth, though there is a determined edge to her whenever someone around her is in danger.

### 🌎 Background

Rowan was born and raised in **[[Black Forge]]**, the great dwarven city carved into the mountains of [[Iornveil]]. Her family tended a small temple and its sacred flame, where Rowan grew up studying the teachings of **[[Lathander]]** and helping miners, craftsmen, travelers, and anyone else who came seeking aid.

Her connection to the divine became impossible to ignore after a mining accident trapped several workers deep beneath the mountain. Rowan's prayers produced a brilliant light that helped rescuers find a safe path to the survivors, inspiring her to devote herself fully to the life of a cleric.

After spending her entire life beneath the mountains, Rowan eventually left Black Forge for the first time and traveled to **[[Solcrest]]** for the royal wedding of [[Princess Seleni]] and [[King Elon]]. What began as a journey representing her faith has become her first true opportunity to discover the wider Sundered Isles.
