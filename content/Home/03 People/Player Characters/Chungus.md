*Half-Elf* • **Ranger**

Chungus is a quiet, observant half-elf from the forests of [[Eladrion]] who seems far more comfortable beneath a canopy of leaves than beneath a crowded roof. Years spent largely alone in the wilderness have made him reserved, but when drawn into conversation he can be surprisingly thoughtful and easy to relate to. He has a fierce respect for nature and little patience for those who destroy it simply because they have the power to do so.

---

### 🧭 Basic Information

- **Homeland:** Refu Forest, Eladrion
- **Age:** 32
- **Ideals:** Protector of free land, natural habitats, and those unable to defend themselves.
- **Affiliation:** None

### 👤 Appearance

Chungus is a 5'8" half-elf with dark tan skin, amber-red hair, and striking scarlet eyes. His clothing and equipment are practical and suited for long periods of travel through deep wilderness rather than cities or noble courts.

He carries himself quietly and deliberately, often watching his surroundings before speaking. His years in isolation are apparent in the way he moves through natural environments with far greater ease than crowded settlements.

### 🌎 Background

Chungus was born beneath the ancient **Mother Oak** in Refu Forest, among a small and largely reclusive community that rarely ventured beyond the woods.

After losing his parents while still young, Chungus spent much of his life alone in the wilderness, finding companionship among animals and the forest itself. Years of solitude, meditation, and survival shaped him into a skilled ranger with a deep connection to the natural world.

Eventually, Chungus chose to leave the safety of his familiar woods and experience more of the world beyond Refu Forest. Though he remains fiercely independent and avoids organizations that would restrict his freedom, he has begun allowing other people into a life that was once almost entirely solitary.
