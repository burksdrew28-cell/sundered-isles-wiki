_Half Elf •_ **_Ranger_**

Chungus is a quiet, observant half-elf ranger who has spent much of his life in solitude, leaving him thoughtful, introspective, and slow to speak. Though he can come across as arrogant or overly self-assured, he is fiercely protective of nature, free lands, and peaceful people threatened by those with greater power.

---

### 🧭 Basic Information

- **Homeland:** [[Refu Forest]], [[Eladrion]]
    
- **Ideals:** Protector of free land and nature.
    
- **Affiliation:** None
    

### 👤 Appearance

Chungus is a 32-year-old half-elf with dark tan skin, amber-red hair, and striking scarlet eyes. He stands 5'8" tall with a medium build, weighing around 165 lbs.

### 🌎 Background

Chungus lived as a hermit in the deep woods and mountains, spending years in solitude, introspection, and meditation. He feels most at home surrounded by nature and holds a particularly strong connection to the [[Mother Oak]], where he was born. His most treasured possession is an acorn passed down to him by his parents from the [[Mother Oak]] itself.

His life is driven by a fierce desire to protect free land, natural habitats, and peaceful peoples from powerful magic users who would dominate them.
