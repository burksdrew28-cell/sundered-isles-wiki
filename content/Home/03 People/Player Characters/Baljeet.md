*Gnome* • **Bard**

Baljeet is an ancient gnome with an unsettling presence and very little interest in explaining where it comes from. Quiet about his past but fearless when confronted by danger, he carries himself like someone who has already survived things far worse than whatever happens to be standing in front of him. Whatever follows Baljeet, he seems determined that nobody else will become its victim.

---

### 🧭 Basic Information

- **Homeland:** Unknown
- **Age:** 300
- **Ideals:** Face evil rather than flee from it, and protect others from becoming victims.
- **Affiliation:** None

### 👤 Appearance

Baljeet is a small gnome standing around 3'5", with brown skin, a bald head, and unusually dark black eyes.

There is something immediately unsettling about him, even when he is doing nothing threatening. He carries the instruments and equipment of a bard, though his presence feels considerably darker than the cheerful wandering musicians found in most taverns.

### 🌎 Background

Very little is publicly known about Baljeet's past, largely because he refuses to speak about it.

What is clear is that he has encountered something terrible during his long life and believes that whatever haunts him is not finished. Baljeet expects danger almost everywhere he goes and places little trust in gods or divine powers.

Rather than hide from what follows him, however, Baljeet appears to actively hunt the darkness that torments him. He refuses to consider himself its victim and is fiercely determined to prevent others from suffering the same fate.
