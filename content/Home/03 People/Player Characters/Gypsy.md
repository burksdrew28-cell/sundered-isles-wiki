*Sea Elf* • **Druid***

Gypsy is a charismatic and endlessly talkative sea elf from the waters surrounding [[Aetherwyn]]. With an adventurous streak, a deep connection to nature, and an almost instinctive understanding of the sea, she tends to approach the unfamiliar with curiosity rather than caution. She is friendly and street-smart, although her stubbornness and occasional clumsiness have a habit of making otherwise simple situations considerably more interesting.

---

### 🧭 Basic Information

- **Homeland:** [[Aetherwyn]], [[Ilayra]]
- **Age:** 90
- **Ideals:** Remaining connected to nature and following wherever its path leads.
- **Affiliation:** None

### 👤 Appearance

Gypsy is a 5'8" sea elf with teal skin covered in distinctive silver markings and bright blue eyes. Her long blonde hair is often worn in loose, bohemian-style braids suited for a life spent near the water.

Her clothing and equipment favor freedom of movement and natural materials, reflecting both her life along the sea and her connection to druidic magic. Even far from the coast, there is something unmistakably oceanic about her appearance.

### 🌎 Background

Gypsy comes from **[[Aetherwyn]]**, where she lived a relatively simple life among the sea elves and became intimately familiar with the surrounding waters. Years spent navigating the Cerulean Sea left her able to read currents, weather, coastlines, and the rhythms of the ocean almost instinctively.

Her connection to nature eventually drew her toward the druidic path, giving form to something she had felt throughout her life.

Eventually, the familiar waters of home were no longer enough. Curious about what existed beyond the shores she knew so well, Gypsy set out to see more of the Sundered Isles, trading the certainty of the sea for adventure on unfamiliar shores.
