_Medium humanoid (elf), neutral good — Dockmistress of [[Home/02 Places/Shops & Merchants/Tide’s Edge Marina|Tide’s Edge Marina]]_

## Description

Tall and lithe, Elara has sea-green hair braided with kelp and wooden beads. Faint runic tattoos of waves and leaves mark her pale skin.

## Lore

Born in the shadow of Refu’s oldest oaks, Elara left her family’s grove to learn the ways of tidal trade. Over decades she mastered navigation, negotiation, and enough forest lore to advise travelers which river trails to avoid at dusk.

## Details

- **Skills:** Perception +4, Investigation +5, Persuasion +4
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish
- **Affiliation:** Refu Forest Wardens
- **Fear:** Losing her dock to forest fires
- **Secret:** She once spared a river-spirit hunter in return for a promise to guard her boats.

## Adventure Hook

Fishermen’s nets vanish at night. Elara seeks discreet help to trail the culprit into the forest shadows.
