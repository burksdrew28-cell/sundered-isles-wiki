_Dockmistress of Tide’s Edge_

Elara Setherien is a tall, lithe elf known for her sea-green hair, usually braided with strands of kelp and small wooden beads. Faint runic tattoos shaped like waves and leaves trace across her pale skin, hinting at a life tied equally to forest and water. She is calm, observant, and practical, with the kind of presence that suggests she has spent many years dealing with sailors, merchants, travelers, and troublemakers.

Born near the ancient woods of Refu Forest, Elara eventually left her family’s grove to learn the ways of river and coastal trade. Over the years, she became skilled in navigation, negotiation, and the old trails running between the forest and the water. Today, she serves as Dockmistress of Tide’s Edge and is considered a reliable source of advice for travelers moving through the region.
