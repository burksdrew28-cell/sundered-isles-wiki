_Medium humanoid (dwarf), lawful neutral — Boatwright and dockhand foreman at [[Home/02 Places/Shops & Merchants/Tide’s Edge Marina|Tide’s Edge Marina]]_

## Description

Stout and broad-shouldered, Rorik wears a braided beard streaked with salt and oil. Burn and hammer scars cover his arms.

## Lore

A third-generation boatwright, Rorik’s family has built vessels for Barlop’s traders since before the canal opened. He prides himself on sturdy hulls and seaworthy craftsmanship.

## Details

- **Skill:** Athletics +4
- **Languages:** Common, Dwarvish
- **Affiliation:** Barlop Boatwrights’ Guild
- **Fear:** A malformed hull capsizing in storm waters
- **Secret:** He keeps a hidden workshop beneath the docks.

## Adventure Hook

Rorik needs rare driftwood logs from Refu’s flooded trails and seeks escorts to retrieve them before forest spirits claim the wood.
