*King of Astra Veyra • Lord of Solcrest*

King Elon is the reigning monarch of [[Astra Veyra]] and head of the royal family in [[Solcrest]]. Known for his composed demeanor and commanding presence, he has ruled the kingdom through a period of relative stability and prosperity.

Though often distant from the everyday lives of common citizens, Elon maintains a reputation as a capable and calculating ruler who takes the security of his kingdom extremely seriously.
