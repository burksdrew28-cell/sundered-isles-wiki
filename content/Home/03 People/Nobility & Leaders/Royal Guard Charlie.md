*Royal Guard of Solcrest*

Charlie is one of the many royal guards stationed throughout [[Solcrest]], recognizable by his polished uniform and somewhat more conversational attitude than many of his fellow guards.

He takes his duties seriously, though he is known to be approachable when circumstances allow it. During major celebrations and crowded events, Charlie is frequently assigned wherever the city guard needs another dependable pair of hands.
