*Princess of Astra Veyra • Bride of the Royal Wedding*

Princess Seleni is the daughter of [[King Elon]] and one of the most recognizable members of the royal family. Warm, charismatic, and considerably more approachable than many expect from royalty, she is particularly popular among the citizens of [[Solcrest]].

Her upcoming wedding has transformed Solcrest into a city-wide celebration, drawing travelers, merchants, nobles, performers, and visitors from across the Sundered Isles.
