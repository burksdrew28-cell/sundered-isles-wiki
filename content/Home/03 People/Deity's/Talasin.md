*The Pathwarden*

Talasin is the deity of journeys, transition, clarity, and perseverance. Followers look to Talasin for guidance when leaving behind one chapter of life and stepping into another.

---

- **Domains:** Journeys, Change, Clarity, Endurance
- **Symbol:** A winding mountain path beneath a single star
- **Worshippers:** Pilgrims, travelers, wanderers, monks, and those beginning a new path
