*The Veiled Eye*

Daharsen is the deity of perception, hidden truth, mirages, and the uncertain boundary between what is real and what is merely believed to be real. Followers of Daharsen often seek wisdom by questioning appearances rather than trusting them.

---

- **Domains:** Perception, Truth, Illusion, Mystery
- **Symbol:** An open eye beneath a wavering sun
- **Worshippers:** Seers, pilgrims, scholars, travelers, and those seeking hidden truths
