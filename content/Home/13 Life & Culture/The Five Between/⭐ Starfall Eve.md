**One of [[The Five Between]]**

Starfall Eve is a night of omens, wishes, navigation, and stargazing. Sailors watch the horizon, astronomers observe the moons, and families gather beneath open skies with warm food and lanterns. The holiday is especially important in isolated coastal settlements where generations have navigated by the heavens.

**Interesting Tradition:** People leave a lantern burning outside until sunrise so that “wandering stars can find their way home.”
