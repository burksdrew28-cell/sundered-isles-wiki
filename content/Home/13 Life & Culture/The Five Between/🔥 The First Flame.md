**One of [[The Five Between]]**

The First Flame marks the beginning of the new year. Communities extinguish their hearths and gather around a single ceremonial fire, from which new flames are carried home. The ceremony symbolizes beginning the year together rather than carrying the previous year's troubles forward.

**Interesting Tradition:** **[[Solcrest]]** traditionally lights an enormous ceremonial brazier visible across much of the city.

Stealing a coal from it is illegal.

It is also an extremely popular teenage challenge.
