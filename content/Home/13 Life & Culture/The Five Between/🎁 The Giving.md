**One of [[The Five Between]]**

The Giving takes place as winter approaches and centers around helping neighbors survive the difficult months ahead. Wealthier families donate food, blankets, wood, medicine, and coin, while entire neighborhoods prepare communal meals. Even rival businesses are expected to suspend feuds for the day.

**Interesting Tradition:** Gifts are traditionally given **anonymously whenever possible**. Being caught taking credit for an extravagant donation is considered spectacularly tacky.
