**20 Bloomtide**

The Festival of Blossoms celebrates love, friendship, beauty, and the full arrival of spring. Streets fill with flower garlands, musicians, brightly colored clothing, and stalls selling sweets flavored with petals and fruit. **[[Solcrest]]** is famous for releasing thousands of flower petals from its towers at sunset, covering entire streets in a drifting blanket of color.

**Interesting Tradition:** Giving someone a flower with **five petals** means friendship. Six means admiration. Seven is a declaration of love, which has caused more than a few extremely awkward misunderstandings.
