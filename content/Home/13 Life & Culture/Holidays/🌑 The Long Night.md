**30 Deepnight**

The Long Night is the darkest night of the year, when families gather indoors around fires and tell stories until morning. Ghost tales, legends of monsters, and stories from before **[[The Sundering]]** are especially popular. Most settlements extinguish unnecessary outdoor lights after the final evening bell.

**Interesting Tradition:** Children are warned never to answer someone knocking on the door after the **third bell** unless they already know who stands outside.

Nobody seems entirely certain where that tradition came from.
