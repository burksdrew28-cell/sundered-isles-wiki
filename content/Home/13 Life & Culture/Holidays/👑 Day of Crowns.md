**15 Suncrest**

The Day of Crowns commemorates the ancient founding traditions of kingdoms throughout the Isles and is one of the largest civic celebrations in **[[Solcrest]]**. Royal processions, military displays, tournaments, public feasts, and speeches fill the capital, although smaller settlements often elect a ridiculous ceremonial “king” or “queen” for the day instead.

**Interesting Tradition:** Some villages crown whoever can complete an absurd local challenge. One year, **[[Pugston]]** supposedly crowned a goat.
