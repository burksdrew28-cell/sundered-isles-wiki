**One of [[The Five Between]]**

High Festival is perhaps the least complicated holiday in [[Home/01 World/World Overview/The Sundered Isles|The Sundered Isles]]. It exists primarily for eating, drinking, games, contests, music, performances, and making questionable decisions in public. Cities compete to create increasingly ridiculous spectacles, and travelers often plan entire journeys around attending the largest celebrations.

**Interesting Tradition:** **[[Midhere]]** hosts a massive harbor race involving decorated boats, magical tricks, musicians, and rules that become increasingly optional as the afternoon continues.
