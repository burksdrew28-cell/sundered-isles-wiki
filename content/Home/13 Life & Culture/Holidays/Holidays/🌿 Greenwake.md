**12 Greengale**

Greenwake celebrates the first great burst of growth after winter. Villages decorate doors, bridges, and fields with fresh greenery, while farmers leave the first corner of every newly planted field untouched as an offering to the land. The celebration is particularly lively around the fertile countryside near **[[Crestfall]]**, where locals hold a race through the fields carrying enormous wreaths without letting them touch the ground.

**Interesting Tradition:** Dropping your Greenwake wreath before finishing the race supposedly guarantees spectacularly bad luck with vegetables for the entire year.
