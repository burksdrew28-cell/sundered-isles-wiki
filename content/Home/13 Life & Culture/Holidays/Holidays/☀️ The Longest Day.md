**30 Highsun**

Celebrated during the height of summer, the Longest Day begins at sunrise and traditionally does not end until the final bonfire burns out. Beaches, plazas, and village squares fill with food, dancing, music, competitions, and enormous fires meant to “carry the sun through the night.” Coastal communities such as **[[Seopon]]** turn the holiday into sprawling waterfront celebrations.

**Interesting Tradition:** Anyone still awake when the final bonfire dies is supposed to make a wish before going to sleep.
