**30 Duskmere**

Shattering Day commemorates one of the legendary final events of **[[The Sundering]]**, though stories about what actually happened vary wildly between cultures. Modern celebrations often involve fireworks, breaking colored glass or crystal replicas, historical performances, and tales of ancient heroes defeating terrible magical powers.

**Interesting Tradition:** Children often receive cheap colored crystal charms and smash them against stones at sunset while making a wish for the coming year.
