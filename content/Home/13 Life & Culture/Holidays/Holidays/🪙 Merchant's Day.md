**22 Goldfall**

Merchant's Day is the busiest trading day of the year, when merchants settle debts, announce new partnerships, and fill marketplaces with special wares brought from across the Sundered Isles. Major crossroads and ports become temporary oceans of tents, banners, performers, exotic foods, and aggressively enthusiastic salespeople.

**Interesting Tradition:** In **[[Canalaton]]**, merchants ring small brass bells whenever they complete an especially large deal. By afternoon, the markets sound completely deranged.
