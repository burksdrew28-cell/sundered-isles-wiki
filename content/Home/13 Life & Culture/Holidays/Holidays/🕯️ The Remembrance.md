**13 Duskmere**

The Remembrance is a quiet holiday dedicated to those who have died. Families light lanterns, visit graves, tell stories of lost relatives, and place small keepsakes beside memorials. In many communities, every household leaves one empty seat at dinner for someone who is no longer there.

**Interesting Tradition:** In **[[Tuph]]**, hundreds of lanterns are placed throughout the surrounding hills, making the entire valley glow after dark.
