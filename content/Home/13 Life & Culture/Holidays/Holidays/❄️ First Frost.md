**7 Frostreach**

First Frost marks the traditional arrival of winter. Families prepare food stores, hunters return from their final major expeditions, and communities gather for warm meals before the coldest months begin. Northern settlements near places such as **[[Ashvale]]** take the celebration particularly seriously.

**Interesting Tradition:** The first person to discover naturally formed ice that morning receives a free drink everywhere in town for the rest of the day.

This has led to surprisingly intense competitive frost hunting.
