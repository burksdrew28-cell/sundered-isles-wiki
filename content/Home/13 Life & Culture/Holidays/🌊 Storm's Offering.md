**18 Stormcall**

Storm's Offering is observed throughout the Isles by sailors, fishermen, merchants, and anyone sensible enough to respect the sea. Small gifts are thrown into the water in hopes that the ocean will accept the offering instead of taking something more valuable during the storm season. Port cities such as **[[Doge Town]]** launch hundreds of tiny candlelit boats into the harbor after sunset.

**Interesting Tradition:** Pirates traditionally offer rum. Sailors claim the sea prefers the expensive stuff.
