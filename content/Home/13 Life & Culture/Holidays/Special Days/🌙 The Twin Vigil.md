When both moons reach fullness together, people across [[Home/01 World/World Overview/The Sundered Isles|The Sundered Isles]] stay awake beneath the strange silver-and-copper light. Temples hold midnight services, astronomers drag enormous instruments into public plazas, and taverns conveniently discover several new varieties of moon-themed alcohol. Different cultures consider the Twin Vigil either extraordinarily lucky or deeply unsettling.

**Interesting Tradition:** In some coastal communities, people place two coins beside each other on a windowsill overnight. If they're found separated in the morning, you're supposedly destined to travel somewhere unexpected before the next Twin Vigil.
