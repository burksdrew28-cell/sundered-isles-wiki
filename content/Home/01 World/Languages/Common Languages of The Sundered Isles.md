These are the languages most commonly encountered throughout the known Sundered Isles:

- **Common**  
    The primary trade language of the Sundered Isles. Spoken in nearly every major port, kingdom, market, and settlement.
    
- **Elvish**  
    Common among elven communities, particularly throughout [[Ilayra]], [[Aetherwyn]], and other ancient elven settlements.
    
- **Dwarvish**  
    The dominant language of the dwarven settlements of [[Iornveil]], especially [[Black Forge]] and the surrounding mountain communities.
    
- **Orcish**  
    Widely spoken among orc communities such as [[Morgruk]], [[Gronkar]], and the distant city of [[Bloodpeak]].
    
- **Halfling**  
    Found primarily in smaller rural communities and secluded settlements throughout the Isles.
    
- **Gnomish**  
    Less widespread than Common, but frequently heard among craftsmen, inventors, scholars, merchants, and gnomish communities.
    
- **Goblin**  
    Spoken by goblins and related peoples scattered throughout the wilderness, ruins, mountains, and less-governed regions of the Isles.
    
- **Giant**  
    An old language still spoken by giants and certain mountain-dwelling peoples. Ancient inscriptions in Giant can occasionally be found in forgotten places.
    
- **Draconic**  
    An ancient language associated with dragons, dragonborn, magic, and very old ruins. Scholars frequently study it even if they rarely speak it conversationally.
    
- **Sylvan**  
    The language of the Fey and creatures strongly connected to the natural and supernatural world. It is uncommon among ordinary people but survives among druids, certain elves, and isolated forest communities.
    
- **Undercommon**  
    A trade language of subterranean peoples and settlements. Rare on the surface, though travelers occasionally hear it in underground markets, ancient tunnels, and less reputable corners of civilization.
    
- **Primordial**  
    An ancient elemental tongue encompassing dialects such as Aquan, Auran, Ignan, and Terran. Rarely spoken in everyday life but known by some scholars, elemental beings, and magically inclined individuals.
