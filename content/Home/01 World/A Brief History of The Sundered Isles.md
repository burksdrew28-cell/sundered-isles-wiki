The [[Sundered Isles]] are an ancient collection of continents, kingdoms, and scattered islands whose history stretches back thousands of years. The modern age began in **Year 0 AS**, following a devastating conflict remembered simply as **[[The Sundering]]**, an event said to have reshaped portions of the world and forever changed the course of its civilizations. In the **6,174 years since**, kingdoms have risen and fallen, cities have been swallowed by wilderness and sand, trade routes have crossed vast oceans, and old ruins have become little more than half-remembered pieces of a forgotten age. Today, most people concern themselves more with the rivalries, cultures, dangers, and opportunities of the modern Isles than with whatever truths may still lie buried beneath them.
