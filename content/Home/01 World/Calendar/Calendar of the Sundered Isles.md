# 📅 Calendar of the Sundered Isles

> *A year of tides, festivals, falling stars, and the occasional historically inconvenient disaster.*

Most of the Sundered Isles use the same common calendar.

The current year is:

> ## **6174 AS**

**AS** means **After the Sundering**.

---

## 🗓️ The Year

A year contains:

- **12 months**
- **30 days per month**
- **5 special days**
- **365 days total**
- **7 days per week**

- 🔥 [[The First Flame]]
- ☀️ [[High Festival]]

These days are celebrated throughout the Isles in different ways depending on region and culture.

# 🌙 The Moons

Two moons watch over the Sundered Isles.

## 🌕 Aestra
The larger silver-white moon.

**Cycle:** 30 days

## 🔴 Vael
A smaller copper-red moon.

**Cycle:** 45 days

When both moons are full together, the event is known as:

> **The Twin Vigil**
