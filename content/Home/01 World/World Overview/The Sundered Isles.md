The [[The Sundered Isles]] are a scattered realm of continents, island chains, and ancient territories separated by vast seas. Though the peoples of these lands share a common history, each region has developed its own cultures, traditions, and dangers. In the northwest, [[Eladrion]] is a stretch of rugged land of ancient forests, misty valleys, and snow-capped peaks. Elven kingdoms and secluded settlements cling to the mountains and highlands, guarding traditions that predate many of the younger civilizations. Across the sea to the northeast lies the volcanic isles [[Cindakar]], a harsh land shaped by fire and stone. Active volcanoes dominate the landscape, while the surrounding waters are notoriously dangerous. Beneath its blackened peaks lie settlements, ruins, and secrets that few outsiders have ever ventured far enough to uncover.

South of the volcanic region stands [[Iornveil]], a vast mountainous continent dominated by dwarven kingdoms, fortified holds, and sprawling underground settlements. Its mountains are rich with ore and precious metals, making it one of the greatest centers of industry and craftsmanship in the known world. Far to the southwest lies [[Kharos]], where endless dunes, ancient ruins, and isolated settlements stretch beneath an unforgiving sun. In contrast, the islands of the [[Ilyara]] are lush, humid, and teeming with strange wildlife, dense jungles, and cultures built around the sea. To the west sits the mysterious [[Velora]], a long, isolated island believed to have been largely untouched by the great civilizations of the world. Its dense wilderness and strange ruins have made it a destination for explorers, treasure hunters, and those foolish enough to believe every old sailor's tale.

At the heart of the Sundered Isles lies the [[Astra Veyra]], home to [[Solcrest]], the great capital and political heart of the realm. Fertile plains, forests, rivers, and major trade routes make this region the crossroads of civilization. People from nearly every corner of the Isles can be found here, bringing with them their languages, religions, rivalries, and ambitions. Yet despite the apparent unity of the realm, tensions simmer beneath the surface. Ancient places remain unexplored, old powers have not entirely disappeared, and the seas between the Isles hold far more than ships and storms. **The Sundered Isles may appear to be a world divided by water, but the threads connecting its peoples run deeper than anyone yet realizes.**
