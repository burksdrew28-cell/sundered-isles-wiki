The **[[Aetheric Ocean]]** encompasses much of the northwestern half of [[The Sundered Isles]], surrounding the continents and islands scattered throughout that region. Known for its enormous open waters, powerful currents, cold northern reaches, and heavily traveled shipping routes, the Aetheric connects many of the Isles’ oldest ports and settlements. Sailors have crossed it for thousands of years, though enormous stretches remain rarely traveled, and old maritime legends insist there are places far from land where the ocean behaves very differently than it should.
