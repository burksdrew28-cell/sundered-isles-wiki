The **[[Luminara Ocean]]** stretches across much of the southeastern half of [[The Sundered Isles]], gradually becoming warmer as it reaches the southern continents. Its waters surround vast stretches of [[Velora]], [[Kharos]], and the island chains farther south, carrying merchants and travelers between some of the most distant regions of the Isles. The Luminara is famous for brilliant sunsets and unusually luminous waters in certain regions, where countless tiny organisms cause ships’ wakes to glow beneath the night sky.
