Jutting dramatically from [[Velora]]’s western coast, the **Hammer Head Peninsula** earns its name from its distinctive shape when viewed on maps. Local legends tell of enormous hammerhead sharks that once gathered around the peninsula in numbers so great that sailors refused to enter the surrounding waters, and carved shark symbols can still be found on old stones throughout the region. Whether the stories are true hardly matters anymore, because the hammerhead has become one of western [[Velora]]’s most recognizable symbols.
