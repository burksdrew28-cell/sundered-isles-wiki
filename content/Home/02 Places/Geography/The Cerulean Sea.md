Nestled within the warmer southern reaches of the [[Luminara Sea]], the **[[Cerulean Sea]]** surrounds the tropical islands of [[Ilayra]]. Its clear turquoise waters are filled with coral reefs, colorful marine life, shallow lagoons, sudden tropical storms, and hundreds of tiny unnamed islands and sandbars. Ilayran sailors know the Cerulean intimately, navigating passages through reefs that outsiders often struggle to even recognize as routes.
