Towering over [[Cindakar]], **[[Rakadnic Volcano]]** is a colossal active volcano whose slopes dominate much of the continent. Smoke constantly drifts from its summit, while lava fields, volcanic caves, ash-covered valleys, and steaming fissures spread throughout the surrounding wilderness. Small eruptions are common enough that the inhabitants of [[Bloodpeak]] reportedly distinguish between dozens of different kinds of volcanic rumbling.
