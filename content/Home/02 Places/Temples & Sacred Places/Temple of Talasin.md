High within the **[[Pow Mountains]]** stands the **Temple of Talasin**, a secluded sanctuary reached by winding mountain paths and steep stone stairways. Pilgrims visit the temple seeking guidance, clarity, and strength during major transitions in their lives, while bells hanging along the mountain paths ring constantly in the powerful winds sweeping across [[Velori]].

---

- **Location:** [[Pow Mountains]]
- **Associated Faith / Deity:** [[Talasin]]
- **Known For:** Pilgrimage, meditation, mountain rites, blessings for journeys
