Deep within **[[Black Forge]]**, where the mountain city’s furnaces cast an almost permanent glow across the stone, stands the **Temple of First Light**, a sanctuary devoted to **[[Lathander]]**, the Morninglord. The temple is built around a sacred flame said to have burned continuously for generations, tended by priests and acolytes who offer healing, guidance, and blessings to miners, craftspeople, travelers, and families throughout the city. Many residents visit before beginning dangerous work beneath the mountains, while others come simply to sit beneath the warm golden light and pray for renewal, courage, or a new beginning.

---

- **Location:** Black Forge, Iornveil
- **Associated Faith / Deity:** Lathander
- **Known For:** Sacred flame, healing, blessings, miners and travelers seeking protection
