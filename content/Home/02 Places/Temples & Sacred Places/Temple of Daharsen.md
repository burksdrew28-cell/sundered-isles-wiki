The **[[Temple of Daharsen]]** stands upon tiny **[[Daharsen Isle]]** just off the coast from [[Starfall]], though “stands” may be an overly confident description. On some days the temple is clearly visible across the water, while on others the island appears to vanish entirely into the heat haze despite calm weather and clear skies.

Those who successfully reach the temple describe pale stone halls, open courtyards, and shrines dedicated to perception, truth, and the uncertainty of what the eyes can see. Boats attempting the journey occasionally return to shore without their passengers remembering whether they ever actually arrived.
