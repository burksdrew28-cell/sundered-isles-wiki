High above the sea on the cliffs of the **[[Seop Peninsula]]** stands the isolated **[[Temple of Salandine]]**, a sacred complex exposed to constant ocean wind and crashing surf far below. Reaching it requires either a long overland pilgrimage from [[Seopon]] or a dangerous climb directly up the coastal cliffs, a journey some followers believe is an important part of visiting the temple.

---

- **Location:** [[Seo]]
    
- **Associated Faith / Deity:** [[Salandine]]
    
- **Known For:** Pilgrimages, cliffside rites, ocean blessings, endurance
