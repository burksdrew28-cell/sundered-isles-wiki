**[[Cindakar]]** is a small, volcanic continent in the far northeastern reaches of [[The Sundered Isles]], isolated from the rest of the world by the treacherous waters of [[The Nyxal Trench Sea]]. Jagged volcanic ridges, blackened valleys, unstable cliffs, steaming vents, and fields of hardened lava make overland travel exceptionally dangerous, while the colossal [[Rakadnic Volcano]] dominates nearly every view of the interior. Only one permanent civilization is widely known to exist here: the remote orc city of [[Bloodpeak]], tucked into the continent’s northeastern reaches. Between the violent terrain, dangerous seas, and constant volcanic activity, Cindakar remains one of the least explored regions in all of [[The Sundered Isles]].
