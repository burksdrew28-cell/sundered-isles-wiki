**[[Astra Veyra]]** is the bustling heartland of the Sundered Isles, a diverse continent of fertile river valleys, northern mountains, mist-covered marshes, rolling farmland, and warm southern shores. At its center sits **[[Solcrest]]**, the royal capital and one of the greatest economic hubs in the Isles, while ports such as Indo and [[Tulin]] connect [[Astra Veyra]] to distant lands and smaller farming communities keep its markets overflowing. Humans are common throughout the continent, though elven settlements, orcish communities, sailors, merchants, farmers, craftsmen, and travelers from nearly every corner of the Isles call it home. From the mysterious depths of [[The Marshlands]] and the peaks of [[The Mesai Mountains]] to the crowded roads surrounding [[Solcrest]], [[Astra Veyra]] is a land where civilization, commerce, wilderness, and old mysteries are never very far apart.
