Eladrion is a vast mountainous highland continent of cold lakes, steep valleys, windswept plateaus, ancient forests, and rugged coastal cliffs. Its people are shaped by the land itself, with hardy mountain communities, isolated villages, ancient elven strongholds, trade towns, and settlements carved directly into the mountains. The continent is home to some of the oldest places in the Sundered Isles, including the quiet island city of Aloriea, the sprawling Refu Forest, and the immense Mansa Plateau that dominates much of eastern Eladrion. Though well settled in certain regions, enormous stretches remain wild, difficult to cross, and scattered with forgotten ruins, strange lakes, old fortresses, and paths leading into places rarely visited.
