**Velora** is one of the largest and most geographically diverse continents in [[The Sundered Isles]], stretching from humid southern peninsulas and warm coastal forests to cooler northern plains, mountains, great lakes, and rugged shorelines. Its immense size has allowed wildly different cultures to develop across the continent, from the elves of [[Lunaris]] and the hardened fishermen of [[Salton]] to the growing melting pot of [[Canalaton]] and the secretive people of [[Bantan]]. Trade, fishing, travel, and migration shape much of Veloran life, especially since the construction of the [[Velora Canal ]]changed how ships move across the continent.

Off [[Velora]]’s eastern coast lies **[[Velori]]**, a large mountainous island believed to have been torn from the mainland during [[The Sundering]]. The island remains culturally distinct from [[Velora]] itself, home to isolated valleys, towering mountains, old forts, sacred places, and the unusual city of [[Pugston]]. Across both mainland and island, [[Velora]] is filled with strange ruins, old pirate history, unexplained phenomena, and enough coastline that nearly every region has its own relationship with the sea.
