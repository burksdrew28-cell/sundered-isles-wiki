**[[Kharos]]** is a harsh and mysterious desert continent of [[The Sundered Isles]], dominated by the vast **[[White Dunes]]**, where wind, heat, and shifting sands. Along its coasts lie weathered ports and quiet settlements, while deeper inland, cities have adapted to brutal heat, scarcity, and isolation in wildly different ways. The northern **[[Sladge Mountains]]** provide a rocky contrast to the endless dunes, and ancient pyramids, melted ruins, strange temples, and half-buried monuments suggest that Kharos has been inhabited far longer than most surviving records can explain.
