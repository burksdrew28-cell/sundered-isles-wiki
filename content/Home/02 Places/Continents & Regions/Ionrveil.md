**Iornveil** is a rugged dwarven homeland northeast of [[Astra Veyra]], dominated by towering mountains, rolling northern hills, deep lakes, mineral-rich valleys, and settlements built as much beneath the earth as upon it. Dwarven culture shapes nearly every corner of the continent, from the immense underground capital of **[[Black Forge]]** and the industrial stoneworks of **[[Karka Dur]]** to isolated villages scattered throughout the highlands. Mining, masonry, smithing, engineering, shipping, and ancient traditions remain central to life here, while remnants of older ages still linger among its mountains. [[Iornveil]] may appear harsh and unwelcoming from afar, but beneath all that stone is one of the most industrious and tightly connected cultures in [[Home/01 World/World Overview/The Sundered Isles|The Sundered Isles]].
