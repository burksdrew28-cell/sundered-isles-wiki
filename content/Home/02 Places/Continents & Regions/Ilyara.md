**[[Ilayra]]** is the southernmost continent of [[The Sundered Isles]], a sprawling tropical archipelago of lush islands, warm turquoise seas, coral-lined coasts, dense jungle, white beaches, and towering palms. Life throughout [[Ilayra]] is deeply tied to the ocean, with fishing, sailing, trade, diving, and island travel shaping nearly every settlement. The wealthy capital of **[[Seopon]]**, the lively streets of **[[Doge Town]]**, and the ancient elven city of **[[Aetherwyn]]** represent wildly different sides of Ilayran culture, while dozens of smaller islands remain sparsely settled or completely wild. Despite its paradise-like appearance, [[Ilayra]] is filled with unexplained ruins, strange creatures, forgotten outposts, and hidden springs.
