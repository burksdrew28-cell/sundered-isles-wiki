Standing atop a massive grassy foothill north of [[The Marshlands]], **[[Fort Lancasir]]** is an old military fortress surrounded by hills and sweeping views of western [[Astra Veyra]]. The fort is best known as a training ground for soldiers and mounted patrols, with the slopes surrounding it used for drills, competitions, and large-scale exercises. Its hilltop position makes its banners visible from miles away, and locals often use the fort’s towers as a landmark when navigating [[The Marshlands]].
