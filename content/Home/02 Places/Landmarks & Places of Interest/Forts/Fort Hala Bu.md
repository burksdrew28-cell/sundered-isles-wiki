Once a notorious pirate stronghold, **[[Fort Hala Bu]]** has since been transformed into an unusual sanctuary for injured, abandoned, and exotic animals. Its old battlements now hold aviaries and climbing enclosures, while former storage chambers have become shelters for creatures from across [[Home/01 World/World Overview/The Sundered Isles|The Sundered Isles]]. A few cannon ports still remain, mostly because nobody has figured out how to convince the larger animals not to sleep in them.
