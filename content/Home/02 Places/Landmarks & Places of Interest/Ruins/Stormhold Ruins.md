Standing amid the harsh lands of **[[Kharos]]**, the **Stormhold Ruins** are all that remain of the former home of **[[House Stormwind]]**. Once a thriving settlement, Stormhold was destroyed by orc clans from **[[Gronkar]]**, leaving shattered stone walls, burned structures, and abandoned streets in its wake. Unlike many ruins across the Sundered Isles, the destruction here is recent enough that traces of the lives once lived within still remain.
