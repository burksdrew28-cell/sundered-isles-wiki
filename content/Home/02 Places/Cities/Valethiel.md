Near the western mountains, where the waters of the **[[Provon River]]** first descend into [[Astra Veyra]], lies **[[Valethiel]]**, a small elven city built among wooded foothills and clear mountain streams. Originally established as an outpost guarding the river’s source and the nearby passes, [[Valethiel]] has gradually grown into a peaceful settlement of rangers, hunters, herbalists, scouts, and craftspeople. Elves remain the majority, though outsiders are welcomed far more openly here than in many older elven communities.

[[Valethiel]] is known for its beautiful wooden architecture, suspended walkways, small shrines, and carefully maintained trails leading into the surrounding mountains. Travelers frequently stop here before attempting journeys farther into the high country, where local guides are valued nearly as much as good boots. Though modest compared with the great cities of [[Astra Veyra]], [[Valethiel]] enjoys a reputation as one of the safest places to begin an unsafe expedition.
