On the far eastern coast of [[Astra Veyra]] sits **[[Tulin]]**, a bustling city famous for one thing above all else: getting people from one place to another. Shipwrights, carriage makers, wheelwrights, engineers, sailmakers, animal breeders, and inventors fill the city’s workshops, producing nearly every form of transportation used throughout [[The Sundered Isles]]. If something rolls, sails, glides, floats, or somehow manages to remain airborne longer than expected, there is a decent chance someone in [[Tulin]] had a hand in building it.

The city itself is constantly in motion. Half-finished vessels line the docks, experimental wagons rattle through testing yards, and strange mechanical contraptions occasionally appear on the streets before disappearing again after an unfortunate encounter with a wall. [[Tulin]]’s craftspeople are fiercely competitive, and arguments over designs, speed, durability, and whose creation is least likely to explode are considered perfectly ordinary conversation.
