Once one of [[Iornveil]]’s great cities, **Tuph** has slowly fallen into decline as younger generations leave in search of work, adventure, and opportunity elsewhere. Broad streets and enormous stone homes built for generations of families now feel strangely empty, while workshops that once employed dozens may be operated by a single aging craftsperson. Tuph remains beautiful and well maintained, but there is an unmistakable stillness hanging over the city.

Those who remain are fiercely proud of their home and have little patience for anyone calling Tuph a dying city. Old traditions are preserved here with unusual devotion, and festivals that once filled entire districts are still held even when attendance barely fills a single square. Visitors often leave with the strange feeling that Tuph is patiently waiting for something, though nobody agrees on what.
