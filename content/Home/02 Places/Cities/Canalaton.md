Built at one of the main entrances to the **[[Velora Canal]]**, **Canalaton** is one of the youngest major cities in [[The Sundered Isles]]. The settlement began as a worker camp during construction of the canal and officially became a city following its completion in **6128 AS**. Workers, engineers, sailors, traders, merchants, and their families remained behind, quickly turning the temporary settlement into a permanent community.

As a result, Canalaton has become one of Velora’s greatest cultural melting pots. Foods, languages, traditions, music, and goods from across the Isles collide along its busy docks and narrow streets. The city is growing so quickly that maps seem to become outdated before the ink dries, and residents are extremely proud that their city was built not by kings or ancient rulers, but by workers with shovels, ropes, stone, and an unreasonable amount of determination.
