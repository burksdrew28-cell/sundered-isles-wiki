At the heart of [[Astra Veyra]] stands **[[Solcrest]]**, the capital of [[The Sundered Isles]] and one of its greatest economic centers. Though only a medium-sized city, [[Solcrest]] somehow manages to contain nearly everything a traveler could hope to find. Built between the two branches of the **[[Provon River]]**, its streets are packed with markets, taverns, guildhalls, temples, workshops, theaters, inns, and an astonishing variety of shops. Merchants from across the Isles come here to buy, sell, negotiate, and occasionally discover that someone is already selling the incredibly specific thing they were looking for.

To the north, the **[[Royal Castle]]** rises from a broad hill overlooking the city, visible from nearly every major street below. [[Solcrest]] is heavily fortified, with guarded gates, thick walls, river defenses, and a strong military presence protecting both the [[Royal Family]] and the wealth flowing through the capital. Beyond the walls lie wide stretches of farmland that supply the city and surrounding settlements. [[Solcrest]] may not dominate the horizon through sheer size, but economically, its influence stretches across nearly every corner of [[The Sundered Isles.]]
