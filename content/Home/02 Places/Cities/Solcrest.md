At the heart of [[Astra Veyra]] stands **Solcrest**, the sprawling capital of [[The Sundered Isles]] and one of the busiest cities anywhere in the known world. Built between the two branches of the **[[Provon River]]**, the city is packed with markets, taverns, guildhalls, temples, workshops, theaters, inns, and shops selling nearly anything a traveler could think to ask for. Its streets are rarely quiet, its plazas are almost always crowded, and merchants from across the Isles arrive daily hoping to make their fortune beneath Solcrest’s high stone walls.

To the north, the **Royal Castle** rises from a broad hill overlooking the city, visible from nearly every major street below. Solcrest is heavily fortified, with guarded gates, thick walls, river defenses, and a considerable military presence protecting both the royal family and the wealth flowing through the capital. Beyond the walls, the landscape opens into wide stretches of farmland that feed the enormous population within. For many travelers, Solcrest is the first place where the Sundered Isles seem impossibly large.
