Built along the warm shores of **[[Dojani]]**, **[[Doge Town]]** is a sprawling, energetic island city where brightly painted buildings crowd together beneath palm trees and steep green hills. Markets spill into streets filled with music, food vendors, fishing carts, sailors, fruit sellers, crowded balconies, and people loudly conducting several conversations at once. The harbor is the heart of the city, packed with fishing boats, merchant vessels, ferries, and questionable craft held together by optimism.

[[Doge Town]] has a reputation for being chaotic, welcoming, and almost impossible to experience quietly. Music carries through neighborhoods long after sunset, street food can be found on nearly every corner, and local markets are famous for bargaining that occasionally becomes a theatrical performance. The city may appear disorderly to outsiders, but residents insist everything works perfectly well once you stop expecting anything to happen in the order you imagined.
