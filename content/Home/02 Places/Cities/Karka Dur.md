**Karka Dur** is a sprawling dwarven industrial city built around enormous quarries, stoneworks, kilns, and masonry yards. While Black Forge is celebrated for weapons and metalcraft, Karka Dur has earned its reputation through stone. Blocks, columns, tiles, statues, bricks, and building materials produced here can be found in cities throughout nearly every continent of [[The Sundered Isles]].

Generations of masons, architects, quarry workers, and stonecutters have transformed the surrounding mountains into carefully managed terraces and excavation sites. Karka Dur craftsmen are famously particular about their work, and a mason may spend twenty minutes explaining why the stone in your house is wrong before remembering you never asked. Nevertheless, when something needs to survive for a thousand years, people tend to call Karka Dur.
