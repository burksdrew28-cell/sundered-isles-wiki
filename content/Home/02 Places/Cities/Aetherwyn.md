On the isolated island of **[[Aethara]]** lies **[[Aetherwyn]]**, an ancient elven city unlike any other in [[The Sundered Isles]]. According to surviving tradition, much of the city once rested beneath the ocean before rising above the waves during [[The Sundering]]. Its architecture still carries that history, with coral-covered walls, shell-encrusted arches, smooth stone shaped by centuries underwater, and ancient structures that resemble reefs more than buildings.

The elves of [[Aetherwyn]] live a famously relaxed life centered around music, community, the sea, storytelling, art, and simply enjoying the day they were given. Bright clothing, braided hair, drums, hammocks, ocean gardens, and long communal meals are common throughout the city. Outsiders are usually welcomed warmly, though anyone expecting punctuality will quickly discover that [[Aetherwyn]] recognizes clocks primarily as decorative objects.
