Resting along the wooded shores of a highland lake, **[[Thornwick]]** is a peaceful village surrounded by dense thickets, flowering briars, and misty hills. Rather than fishing heavily, the people of [[Thornwick]] are known for cultivating medicinal herbs, berries, dyes, and unusual plants that thrive in the cool lakeside climate. Small gardens cover nearly every patch of open ground, and woven thorn fences divide the village into winding little paths. Local tradition holds that cutting down a living thorn hedge without planting another brings terrible luck, so the village has gradually become a beautiful, slightly inconvenient maze.
