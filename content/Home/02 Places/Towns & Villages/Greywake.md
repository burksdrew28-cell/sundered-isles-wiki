Sharing the island of [[Dojani]] with [[Doge Town]], **[[Greywake]]** could hardly feel more different from its loud neighboring city. The village is built almost entirely among enormous coastal palms, with homes, walkways, gathering platforms, and rope bridges suspended high above the beach. Locals descend to the ground mostly to fish, gather supplies, or greet visitors, preferring the breeze and shade of their treetop homes to the heat below.
