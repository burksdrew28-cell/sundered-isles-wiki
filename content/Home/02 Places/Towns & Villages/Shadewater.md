Hidden along a secluded stretch of [[Seo]]’s coastline, **[[Shadewater]]** is a small settlement inhabited almost entirely by extremely wealthy families seeking distance from the rest of [[Ilayra]]. High walls, private docks, tropical estates, guarded roads, and meticulously maintained gardens make the settlement feel more like a collection of private retreats than a village. Outsiders are tolerated when invited, employed, or delivering something expensive, and generally discouraged under every other circumstance.
