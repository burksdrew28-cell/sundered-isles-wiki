Located in northern [[Velora]], **Willowrest** occupies a rare stretch of land where travelers can reach either coast within a relatively short journey. The village has become a natural stopping point for merchants, messengers, and travelers crossing the continent, with shaded roads, a modest inn, and enormous willow trees providing rest along the way. Locals pride themselves on being excellent hosts and even better sources of road gossip.
