On Kharos’s eastern coast sits **[[Starfall]]**, a small settlement built around a field of smooth black stones scattered throughout the surrounding pale sand. Local tradition claims the stones fell from the heavens centuries ago, and craftspeople polish fragments into jewelry, charms, and navigation markers that shimmer faintly beneath moonlight. On certain nights, residents gather along the beach to watch unusually bright streaks of light cross the eastern sky.
