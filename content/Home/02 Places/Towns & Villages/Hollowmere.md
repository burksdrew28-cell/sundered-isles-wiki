Near the edge of the **[[Nyxal Trench Sea]]**, **Hollowmere** is widely regarded as the last comfortable place to stop before attempting the dangerous crossing toward volcanic [[Cindakar]]. Inns, supply shops, weather watchers, and veteran sailors make up much of the village, with nearly every building carrying some warning, charm, or superstition concerning the waters beyond. Travelers heading farther north quickly learn that asking a Hollowmere sailor about the Trench is an excellent way to lose an entire evening.
