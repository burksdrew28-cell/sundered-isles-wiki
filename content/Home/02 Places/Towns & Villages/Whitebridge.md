Just outside [[Solcrest]] lies **[[Whitebridge]]**, a prosperous farming village named for the pale stone bridge that crosses one of the region’s irrigation canals. Its fields are known for producing enormous quantities of grain, vegetables, and orchard fruit for the capital, while its weekly market attracts cooks and merchants looking for the freshest produce around. [[Whitebridge]] takes agriculture unusually seriously, and its annual harvest competition has caused arguments between neighboring families that have lasted for generations.
