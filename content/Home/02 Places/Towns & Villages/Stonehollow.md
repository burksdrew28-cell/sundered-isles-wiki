Hidden within a small northern valley on [[Velori]], **Stonehollow** was once known as **Pebble Brook Hollow**, named for the shallow stream running through the village. That changed after the locals discovered, inherited, or perhaps simply became extremely enthusiastic about a mysterious golden statue. Over time, nearly every pebble in the village brook was gathered and stacked around it, eventually forming an enormous monument that now towers above the settlement. Nobody outside Stonehollow seems entirely sure who the statue depicts, but villagers treat the subject with absolute seriousness.
