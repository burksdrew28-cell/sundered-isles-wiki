Along the western coast of [[Kharos]] lies **[[Hollow Sky]]**, a cozy desert village where white dunes meet cool ocean breezes. Low homes are built around shaded courtyards and colorful awnings, while residents fish, gather salt, grow hardy coastal plants, and spend long evenings beneath open skies. The village is famous for woven hammocks and rooftop sleeping platforms, where locals escape the heat and watch stars spread across the desert night.
