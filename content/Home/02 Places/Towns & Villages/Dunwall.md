**[[Dunwall]]** is a quiet agricultural village surrounded by rolling pastureland, sheep fields, and low stone walls stretching across the countryside. The settlement is especially famous for its wool, cheeses, and stubborn livestock, with many families raising the same bloodlines of sheep for centuries. The villagers are friendly enough, but they have an odd habit of naming nearly every animal they own, which makes ordering lamb at the local tavern a surprisingly personal experience.
