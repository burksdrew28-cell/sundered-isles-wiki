Situated roughly halfway along the **[[Velora Canal]]**, **MossBrook** grew from a small waterside settlement into an important rest stop after the canal opened. Barges, merchant vessels, and work crews frequently stop here for supplies, repairs, and food before continuing their journey. Moss-covered locks, old worker cottages, rope bridges, and waterside taverns give the village a slightly ramshackle charm.
