Set among the farmland southwest of [[Solcrest]], **[[Crestfall]]** is known for its enormous fields of colorful flowers, herbs, and specialty crops grown for healers, perfumers, brewers, and alchemists in the capital. During the warmer months the countryside around the village becomes a patchwork of vivid fields visible from miles away. [[Crestfall]] also hosts a lively seasonal festival where farmers decorate wagons, compete with elaborate floral displays, and attempt increasingly ridiculous ways of transporting oversized vegetables through town.
