Resting along the shore of **[[Lake Okamack]]**, **Hearthglen** is a peaceful village known for warm inns, lakeside cottages, orchards, and communal gatherings around enormous outdoor fire pits. Fishing and farming support much of the community, but Hearthglen has also become a favored retreat for travelers seeking somewhere quiet to recover before heading toward [[Velora]]’s mountains.
