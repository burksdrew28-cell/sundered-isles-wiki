Across **[[Lake Okamack]]** from [[Hearthglen]] lies **Pinehaven**, a cool mountain-edge settlement surrounded by dense evergreen forests. Lumber, resin, mountain herbs, and small-scale hunting support the village, while trails from Pinehaven lead toward the higher elevations beyond. The smell of pine smoke hangs permanently in the air, and locals have an almost obsessive knowledge of every trail, tree, and suspiciously shaped rock within a day’s walk
