Just northwest of [[Seopon]] sits **[[Doa Beach]]**, a tiny seaside village of palm-thatched huts, fishing racks, small docks, and boats pulled directly onto the sand. Families here live almost entirely from the ocean, catching fish, gathering shellfish, diving for useful materials, and trading their catch with travelers heading toward the capital. Life is simple, communal, and closely tied to the tides, with half the village seemingly able to predict tomorrow’s weather by staring thoughtfully at the water for thirty seconds.
