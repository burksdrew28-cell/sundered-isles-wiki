Built where several cold mountain rivers spread into a wide delta, **[[Eldercross]]** is a weathered waterside town of raised wooden homes, narrow footbridges, reed-covered banks, and constantly shifting channels. The locals make their living through fishing, ferrying travelers, gathering river plants, and guiding merchants through the maze of waterways before they reach the larger roads of [[Eladrion]]. [[Eldercross]] is especially known for its old stone crossing markers, many of which stand half-submerged throughout the delta and are believed to predate the town itself.
