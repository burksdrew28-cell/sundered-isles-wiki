Far in the northern reaches of [[Eladrion]] lies **[[Greymoor]]**, the northernmost permanently inhabited village in the Sundered Isles. Surrounded by windswept hills, stony moorland, and long stretches of empty wilderness, [[Greymoor]] is a hardy settlement of low stone cottages built to withstand brutal winters and relentless mountain winds. Its people are famously self-reliant, raising shaggy livestock, preserving nearly everything they eat, and maintaining enormous communal fires through the coldest months. Travelers reaching [[Greymoor]] are treated warmly, partly because visitors are rare and partly because everyone wants to hear what is happening in the warmer half of the world.
