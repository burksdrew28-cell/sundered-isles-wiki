*Adventuring Supply Shop*

A bustling corner shop in the heart of Solcrest catering to explorers, mercenaries, and treasure-seekers with dependable gear for the road ahead.

- **Owner:** [[Alana Brightforge]]
- **Location:** [[Solcrest]]
- **Sells:** Adventuring gear, backpacks, rope, torches, bedrolls, waterskins, and travel supplies
- **Services:** Gear repairs, sharpening, pack animal rentals, and local guides
