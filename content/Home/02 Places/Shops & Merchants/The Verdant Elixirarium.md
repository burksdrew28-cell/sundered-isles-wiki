*Apothecary & Herbalist*

A moss-framed herbalist shop filled with dried herbs, bubbling brews, strange roots, and the earthy scent of freshly gathered plants.

- **Owners:** [[Ysolde Fernbloom]] & [[Yecko Quickroot]]
- **Location:** [[Solcrest]]
- **Sells:** Potions, antitoxins, herbal remedies, healer's supplies, and medicinal herbs
- **Services:** Potion crafting, herbal identification, and alchemical lessons
