_Harbor of the Mother Oak’s Blessing_

> Nestled on [[Home/02 Places/Cities/Barlop|Barlop]]’s winding canal, just south of the Refu Forest, Tide’s Edge Marina welcomes merchants, hunters, and wayward travelers seeking passage, supplies, or safe harbor. Weathered docks creak under lantern light, while elven lanterns and driftwood totems carved with forest runes watch over every moored vessel.

## Local Lore

Tide’s Edge opened sixty years ago after Barlop’s merchants petitioned forest wardens to bless a safe canal through Refu’s swampy tail. Travelers still pass beneath wind-whispering oaks carved with warding runes. Locals say forest spirits inspect the boats after dark, ensuring no harm comes to their woods.
