*Scribe & Arcane Goods Shop*

A tidy little shop filled with books, parchment, colorful inks, spell components, and other supplies for scholars and spellcasters.

- **Owner:** [[Meredith Quill]]
- **Location:** [[Solcrest]]
- **Sells:** Ink, parchment, journals, scroll cases, maps, spell components, and arcane goods
- **Services:** Scroll scribing, spellbook copying, and custom maps
