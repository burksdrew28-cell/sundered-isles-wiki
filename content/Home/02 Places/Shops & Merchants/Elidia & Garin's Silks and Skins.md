*Clothier & Leatherworker*

A cozy shop filled with colorful fabrics, sturdy leathers, traveling clothes, and the familiar scents of fresh dye and beeswax polish.

- **Owners:** [[Elidia Faewinter]] & [[Garin Ironhide]]
- **Location:** [[Solcrest]]
- **Sells:** Clothing, cloaks, boots, belts, satchels, fabrics, and leather goods
- **Services:** Clothing repairs, boot resoling, leatherwork, and alterations
