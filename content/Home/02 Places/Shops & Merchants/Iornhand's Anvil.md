*Blacksmith & Armorer*

A sturdy stone-and-timber forge where the constant ringing of hammer on steel draws adventurers, guards, and townsfolk alike.

- **Owner:** [[Bram Ironhand]]
- **Location:** [[Solcrest]]
- **Sells:** Weapons, armor, shields, ammunition, and metal goods
- **Services:** Weapon and armor repairs, sharpening, armor conditioning, and custom engraving
