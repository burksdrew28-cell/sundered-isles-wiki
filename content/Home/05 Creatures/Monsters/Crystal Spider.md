> A small crystalline arachnid that spins shimmering webs through caves, ruins, and crystal growths.

- **Type:** Monstrosity
    
- **Size:** Small
    
- **Found In:** Crystal caves, ruins, underground vaults
    
- **Temperament:** Territorial and aggressive when disturbed
    
- **Danger:** Moderate
    

## Traits

- Camouflages easily among crystal formations
    
- Spins sharp, crystalline webs
    
- Often attacks in groups

- Crystal shards
    
- Crystal silk
