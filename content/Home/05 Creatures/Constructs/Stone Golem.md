>A massive animated guardian carved from ancient stone and awakened by magic.

- **Type:** Construct
    
- **Size:** Large
    
- **Found In:** Ancient ruins, temples, vaults
    
- **Temperament:** Unmoving until disturbed or commanded
    
- **Danger:** High
    

## Traits

- Immensely strong and difficult to damage
    
- Resistant to many ordinary weapons
    
- Follows simple magical commands
    

## Loot / Uses

- Ancient stone fragments
    
- Magical core or rune pieces
