## 📝 What Happened

Before departing for [[Tulin]], [[Sir Pugsington]] gifted the party magical summoning dog bones. Along the way, they welcomed a new companion, [[Blossom]], a timid, pink-skinned gnome sorcerer dressed entirely in pink.

During their journey, the party encountered an eccentric stranger investigating a glowing crack in the earth. He offered them mysterious glowing orbs that absorbed into their skin, granting each adventurer 1,000 XP.

Upon arriving in [[Tulin]], the party searched for passage to [[Doge Town]]. After being rejected by an arrogant captain, they met[[ Captain Red Rus]], a drunken pirate who promised them passage if they recovered his missing "parrot." The parrot turned out to be an orange cat lost in a card game to [[Lenny]] the Flumph. After several unsuccessful attempts, [[Gypsy]] defeated [[Lenny]] and won the cat back.

The following morning, the party traveled with Red Rus to Dead Man's Cove, where they discovered evidence that [[King Elon]] had sailed to [[Deskar]] on 25 Suncrest. While exploring, [[Leontus]], [[Gypsy]], and [[Blossom]] experienced a shared vision of the cove as a pristine, crystalline landscape.

Moments later, orcs ambushed the party and set Red Rus's ship ablaze. After defeating the attackers, Red Rus confessed that he had stolen a mysterious [[Aether Compass]] from the orcs years earlier. The compass had recently begun glowing around the time of [[Shattering Day]].

Red Rus entrusted the compass to the party, claimed the defeated orcs' ship, and set sail with the adventurers toward Doge Town.

## 👥 People Met

- **[[Blossom]]:** A sweet, timid, pink-skinned gnome sorcerer dressed entirely in pink who joined the party on their journey to [[Tulin]].
    
- **Eccentric Stranger:** A mysterious man investigating a glowing crack in the earth who distributed magical orbs to the party.
    
- **[[Lenny]] the [[Flumph]]:** An honest card dealer in [[Tulin]] who had won an orange cat named Parrot in a card game.
    
- **[[Captain Red Rus]]:** A drunken pirate captain who offered the party passage in exchange for recovering his beloved cat.
    
- **Parrot:** Red Rus's beloved orange cat, mistakenly believed to be an actual parrot until Blossom discovered the truth.

- **Road to Tulin:** The party encountered a mysterious stranger investigating a glowing crack in the ground.
    
- **[[Tulin]]:** A bustling port city where the party searched for passage to Doge Town, encountered [[Lenny]] the Flumph, and recovered [[Captain Red Rus]]'s cat.
    
- **Dead Man's Cove:** A secluded coastal cove south of [[Tulin]] where the party discovered clues about [[King Elon]], experienced visions of a crystalline landscape, and survived an orc ambush.

## 🧭 New Leads

- **The Missing King:** [[King Elon]] reportedly sailed to [[Deskar]] on 25 Suncrest, but his reason for traveling there remains unknown.
    
- **The [[Aether Compass]]:** The mysterious compass began glowing around the time of [[Shattering Day]]. Its purpose and connection to the ancient crystals remain unknown.
    
- **The Cove Vision:** [[Leontus]], [[Gypsy]], and [[Blossom]] witnessed a pristine, crystalline version of [[Dead Man's Cove]], raising questions about the land's forgotten past.
    
- **The Mysterious Stranger:** The identity of the eccentric man and the true nature of his magical orbs remain unexplained.
    
- **The Journey to Doge Town:** With [[Captain Red Rus]] now commanding the captured orc ship, the party continues its journey toward [[Ilayra]].
