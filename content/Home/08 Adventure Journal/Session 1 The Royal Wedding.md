## 📝 What Happened

The party arrived in Solcrest to attend Princess Seleni's royal wedding. After an unsuccessful attempt to bribe Captain Charlie, Sir Pugsington helped them gain entry to the city.

During the wedding, a devastating earthquake tore through Solcrest, opening a massive sinkhole beneath the Royal Plaza. The party descended into an ancient underground vault, where they encountered dangerous creatures, mysterious puzzles, and a giant rat that transformed into a hooded woman.

After retrieving an enormous crystal and escaping the vault, the party watched as it ascended into the sky and shattered, scattering glowing fragments across the Sundered Isles.

In the aftermath, a mysterious entity with enormous purple eyes appeared in a shared vision, thanking the party for their actions.

Amid the chaos, King Elon mysteriously disappeared.
